# CUAD pack — Supply Agreement (Bluewater Components / Argent Motors) execution copy

SUPPLY AGREEMENT (EXECUTION COPY)

This Supply Agreement (this "Agreement"), dated as of September 9, 2025, is entered into by and between Bluewater Components LLC, a Michigan limited liability company ("Supplier"), and Argent Motors Corporation, a Nevada corporation ("Buyer").

1. Scope. Supplier shall manufacture and sell to Buyer electro-hydraulic brake actuators, part number family BA-7 (the "Products"), conforming to the specifications in Exhibit A.

2. Minimum Purchase Commitment. Buyer shall purchase not fewer than forty thousand (40,000) units of Products in each Contract Year. If Buyer's purchases fall short of the minimum in any Contract Year, Buyer shall pay Supplier a true-up equal to sixty percent (60%) of the then-current unit price for each unit of shortfall.

3. Forecasts; Volume Threshold. Buyer shall deliver rolling quarterly forecasts, the first month of which is binding. Purchase orders in any quarter exceeding one hundred twenty percent (120%) of the binding forecast require Supplier's prior written consent and are subject to a six percent (6%) expedite surcharge on the excess units.

4. Pricing; Price Restrictions. Unit prices in Exhibit B are firm for the first twenty-four (24) months of the term. Thereafter, Supplier may adjust unit prices no more than once per Contract Year and by no more than the lesser of (a) the percentage change in the Producer Price Index for motor vehicle parts and (b) three percent (3%). Except as expressly stated in this Section 4, Supplier may not increase or decrease prices. No most-favored-customer or most-favored-nation pricing commitment is made under this Agreement.

5. Non-Compete. During the term and for two (2) years following its expiration or termination, Supplier shall not develop, manufacture, or supply brake actuators functionally equivalent to the Products for any passenger-vehicle original equipment manufacturer in North America other than Buyer.

6. Non-Solicitation of Customers. During the term and for one (1) year thereafter, Supplier shall not solicit any member of Buyer's franchised dealer network for direct aftermarket sales of the Products or functionally equivalent goods.

7. Right of First Refusal. Before selling, licensing, or otherwise transferring the BA-7 product line, its tooling, or related manufacturing assets to any third party, Supplier shall first offer such sale or transfer to Buyer on terms no less favorable than those offered to the third party. Buyer shall have forty-five (45) days from receipt of the offer to accept.

8. Change of Control. If Supplier undergoes a change of control — including a merger, sale of a majority of its equity, or a sale of all or substantially all of its assets — Supplier shall give Buyer prompt written notice, and Buyer may terminate this Agreement upon written notice delivered within ninety (90) days after receiving Supplier's notice.

9. Late Delivery; Liquidated Damages. For any shipment delivered more than five (5) business days after the confirmed delivery date, Supplier shall pay Buyer liquidated damages of seventy-five dollars ($75) per unit per week of delay, capped at ten percent (10%) of the value of the affected order. The parties agree this sum is a genuine pre-estimate of Buyer's loss and not a penalty.

10. Insurance. Supplier shall maintain product liability insurance of not less than $5,000,000 per occurrence with an insurer rated A- or better, naming Buyer as an additional insured, and shall furnish certificates of insurance annually.

11. Third-Party Beneficiaries. Buyer's affiliates that purchase Products under this Agreement are express and intended third-party beneficiaries of Sections 9 and 10 and may enforce those Sections directly against Supplier.

12. Books and Records. Supplier shall maintain quality records for seven (7) years. Buyer shall have NO right under this Agreement to audit Supplier's books, records, or facilities; Supplier's compliance is verified solely through part-submission warrants and certificates of conformance. Each party's liability under this Agreement is subject to the remedies expressly stated herein.

13. Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to conflicts-of-law principles.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the date first written above.
