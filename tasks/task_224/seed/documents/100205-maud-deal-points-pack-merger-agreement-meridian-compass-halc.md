# MAUD deal points pack — Merger Agreement (Meridian Compass / Halcyon Therapeutics) execution version — Article II consideration and Article VIII termination excerpt

AGREEMENT AND PLAN OF MERGER, dated as of June 2, 2026 (this "Agreement"), by and among Meridian Compass Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Parent"), MC Falcon Merger Sub, Inc., a Delaware corporation and a wholly owned subsidiary of Parent ("Merger Sub"), and Halcyon Therapeutics, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "Company"). Conformed excerpt of Article II (Merger Consideration) and Article VIII (Termination; Remedies) from the EXECUTION VERSION, prepared by Ellsworth & Crane LLP for the deal-points file. This excerpt supersedes all prior drafts, including draft No. 3 circulated May 11, 2026.

Section 2.1 Merger Consideration. Each share of Company Common Stock issued and outstanding immediately prior to the Effective Time (other than Excluded Shares and Dissenting Shares) shall be converted into the right to receive $28.50 in cash, without interest (the "Merger Consideration"). The Merger is a one-step merger under Section 251 of the DGCL; adoption of this Agreement by the affirmative vote of the holders of a majority of the outstanding shares of Company Common Stock (the "Company Stockholder Approval") is the only vote of Company stockholders required.

Section 8.1 Termination. This Agreement may be terminated at any time prior to the Effective Time: (a) by mutual written consent of Parent and the Company; (b) by either Parent or the Company if the Merger shall not have been consummated on or before March 31, 2027 (the "Outside Date"); provided, that if on such date each condition other than the Regulatory Condition has been satisfied or waived, the Outside Date shall automatically be extended to June 30, 2027; (c) by the Company, prior to receipt of the Company Stockholder Approval, in order to enter into a definitive agreement providing for a Superior Proposal, subject to compliance with Section 5.4 and concurrent payment of the Company Termination Fee; or (d) by Parent, if the Company Board shall have effected an Adverse Recommendation Change.

Section 8.2 Company Termination Fee. In the event this Agreement is terminated pursuant to Section 8.1(c) or Section 8.1(d), the Company shall pay to Parent a fee of $125,000,000 in cash (the "Company Termination Fee"). The Company Termination Fee shall also become payable if (i) an Acquisition Proposal has been publicly disclosed after the date hereof and not withdrawn, (ii) this Agreement is thereafter terminated pursuant to Section 8.1(b) or on account of the Company's uncured material breach, and (iii) within twelve (12) months after such termination (the "Tail Period") the Company consummates, or enters into a definitive agreement providing for, any Acquisition Proposal (with fifty percent (50%) substituted for the twenty percent (20%) thresholds in the definition thereof). In no event shall the Company Termination Fee be payable more than once.

Section 8.3 Parent Termination Fee. In the event this Agreement is terminated (i) by either party pursuant to Section 8.1(b) at a time when the Regulatory Condition has not been satisfied but each other condition has been satisfied or waived, or (ii) by the Company because Parent has failed to consummate the Closing when required by Section 1.2, Parent shall pay to the Company a fee of $265,000,000 in cash (the "Parent Termination Fee"). In no event shall the Company be entitled to receive both the Parent Termination Fee and a grant of specific performance compelling consummation of the Merger.

Section 8.4 Specific Performance. The parties agree that irreparable damage would occur if any provision of this Agreement were not performed in accordance with its specific terms, and that each party shall be entitled to an injunction or injunctions to prevent breaches of this Agreement and to enforce specifically the terms and provisions hereof — including, for the avoidance of doubt, the right of the Company to cause Parent and Merger Sub to consummate the Merger — in each case without proof of damages, this being in addition to any other remedy at law or in equity.
