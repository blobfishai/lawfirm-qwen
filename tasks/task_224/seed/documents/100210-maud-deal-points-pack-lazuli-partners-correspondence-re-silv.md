# MAUD deal points pack — Lazuli Partners correspondence re Silverline termination-fee benchmarking

CORRESPONDENCE — from Lazuli Partners LLC (financial advisor to the Silverline Data Systems special committee) to the deal team distribution list; re: break-fee benchmarking ahead of the July 18, 2026 board meeting. Filed for the record; this letter is advisory only and does not state the terms of any executed agreement.

Team — ahead of Saturday's board session, our benchmarking of company-side termination fees in all-cash technology tender offers signed in the trailing twenty-four months shows a median of approximately 3.4% of fully diluted equity value. Applied to the equity value implied by the price under discussion, that median would imply a fee of roughly $92,000,000, with the interquartile range running from approximately $78,000,000 to $101,000,000. We would characterize anything at or below 3.5% as within the range the Delaware courts have routinely sustained.

On process structures: several recent sponsor-backed deals in the set paired a conventional no-shop with a 35-day go-shop window and a bifurcated fee (roughly half the full fee for a topping bid signed during or shortly after the go-shop). Given the breadth of the pre-signing outreach the committee ran — twenty-one logical buyers contacted under NDA — we do not believe a go-shop is necessary here and expect the buyer to resist one strenuously; whether the executed agreement includes any such construct is a matter for the definitive documentation, which counsel will summarize separately.

We will circulate the full comparables deck under separate cover. Please treat these figures as directional inputs to the committee's deliberation rather than as terms of the transaction.

— R. Okafor, Managing Director, Lazuli Partners LLC
