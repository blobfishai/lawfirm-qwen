Transmitted by Dahlgren & Pike LLP, counsel to Corvid Analytics LLC, 9 July 2026. This rev B counter-draft supersedes in full our rev A transmittal of 26 June 2026; please disregard rev A in its entirety.

TRACKED-CHANGE SUMMARY (Northgale client form v2 -> Corvid rev B):

1. Section 2.2 (Renewal). Client form: automatic renewal for successive one (1) year renewal periods. Rev B: successive two (2) year renewal periods. Rationale: capacity planning for infrastructure dedicated to Northgale workloads.

2. Section 4.3 (Late Amounts). Client form: interest on late undisputed amounts at 1.0% per month. Rev B: 1.5% per month.

3. NEW Section 4.4 (Suspension). Rev B adds: if any undisputed invoice remains unpaid fifteen (15) days after its due date, Provider may suspend Services until all past-due amounts are paid in full.

4. Section 7.2 (Convenience). Client form: Customer may terminate for convenience upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice. Rev B: ninety (90) days' prior written notice.

5. NEW Section 7.4 (Wind-Down). Rev B adds: upon any termination other than for Provider's uncured material breach, a wind-down period of sixty (60) days shall apply, during which Customer pays the full monthly base service fee for wind-down services plus all committed third-party costs.

6. Section 9.1 (Cap). Client form: cap measured at the Fees paid or payable in the twenty-four (24) months preceding the first event giving rise to liability. Rev B: twelve (12) months.

7. Section 9.2 (Carve-Outs). Rev B deletes clause (c), so that gross negligence and willful misconduct are subject to the Section 9.1 cap. Clauses (a) (Confidentiality) and (b) (IP Indemnification) are retained as drafted.

8. Section 11.2 (Credits). Client form: aggregate service-credit cap of 20% of the monthly base service fee per month. Rev B: aggregate cap of 10%.

All other terms of the client form v2 are accepted as drafted. We are available to discuss the above at your convenience. — Dahlgren & Pike LLP
