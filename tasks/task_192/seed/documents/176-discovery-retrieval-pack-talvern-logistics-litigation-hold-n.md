# Discovery-retrieval pack — Talvern Logistics litigation hold notice to ESI custodians (2025-05-06)

TALVERN LOGISTICS GROUP LLC — OFFICE OF THE GENERAL COUNSEL
LITIGATION HOLD NOTICE — PRIVILEGED PORTIONS REDACTED; PRODUCED PURSUANT TO COURT ORDER OF SEPTEMBER 4, 2025
Date of issuance: May 6, 2025
From: S. Marchetti, General Counsel and Secretary
To: Distribution list of eighteen custodians, including D. Kessler (VP Operations), R. Okafor (Site Manager, Complex 4), P. Natarajan (Regional Energy Desk), N. Grieve (CFO)

Re: PRESERVATION OBLIGATION — Meridian Grain Cooperative dispute, Silo Complex 4

You are receiving this notice because the company reasonably anticipates litigation with Meridian Grain Cooperative, Inc. arising from grain storage conditions at Silo Complex 4, Halden Junction, under the Warehousing and Climate Services Agreement dated August 1, 2023. On May 5, 2025 the company received a preservation demand from the cooperative's counsel. Effective immediately upon your receipt of this notice, you must preserve, and must not delete, alter, overwrite or discard, the categories of information described below, in whatever form held.

Categories to preserve: (1) all communications, in any medium including email, chat, text message and voicemail, concerning climate control, dehumidification, humidity, energy usage, load-shedding or maintenance at Silo Complex 4, from January 1, 2025 forward; (2) site diaries, annunciator and alarm logs, controller data, and sensor or data-logger records for Complex 4; (3) documents concerning the Warehousing and Climate Services Agreement, including performance, monitoring and specification compliance; (4) documents concerning cost containment measures affecting leased storage sites; and (5) documents concerning the condition, grading or disposition of grain stored at Complex 4.

IT actions: as of the date of this notice, information technology has been directed to suspend automatic deletion, journaling expiry and mailbox retention cycling for all custodians on the distribution list, and to sequester existing backup media within the covered date range. Custodians must not rely on those systems in place of personal compliance.

This obligation continues until you receive written release from this office. Questions should be directed to the undersigned and not discussed with other custodians. Acknowledgment of receipt is required within three business days by return email.

S. Marchetti
General Counsel and Secretary, Talvern Logistics Group LLC
