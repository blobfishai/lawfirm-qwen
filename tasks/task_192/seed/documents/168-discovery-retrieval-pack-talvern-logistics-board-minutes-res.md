# Discovery-retrieval pack — Talvern Logistics board minutes: Resolution 2025-09, Initiative CC-7 (2025-04-02)

TALVERN LOGISTICS GROUP LLC
MINUTES OF A REGULAR MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
April 2, 2025 — 10:00 AM CT — Talvern headquarters, boardroom A, and by video conference
Bates TLG-BRD-2025-04 — PRODUCED SUBJECT TO PROTECTIVE ORDER, No. 0:25-cv-01877

Present: Chair E. Talvern-Whitcomb; Directors S. Marchetti (also General Counsel and Secretary), H. Bloor, K. Osei, R. Fontaine, L. Adler, J. Whitcomb-Pryce. Also attending for portions: Chief Financial Officer N. Grieve.

1. Call to order; quorum. The Chair called the meeting to order at 10:02 AM and the Secretary confirmed a quorum of seven directors.

2. Approval of prior minutes. The minutes of the February 5, 2025 regular meeting were approved as circulated.

3. First-quarter financial review. CFO Grieve presented the first-quarter management accounts, noting the energy cost overrun previously reported to the Board and management's containment work in response. Discussion followed regarding demand charges at leased storage sites and fleet fuel spend.

4. Cost Containment Initiative CC-7. CFO Grieve presented for adoption the cost containment program designated CC-7, as set out in management memorandum TLG-FIN-22 dated March 28, 2025, previously circulated to all directors. The Chair noted that the memorandum is incorporated into these minutes by reference and its contents are not restated here. Upon motion duly made by Director Bloor and seconded by Director Osei, the Board unanimously adopted the following resolution by a vote of 7-0:

RESOLUTION 2025-09. RESOLVED, that the Cost Containment Initiative designated CC-7, as presented to the Board in management memorandum TLG-FIN-22 dated March 28, 2025, is hereby ADOPTED AND RATIFIED in all respects, including the implementation steps already taken by management under that initiative; and FURTHER RESOLVED, that management is authorized and directed to continue implementation of CC-7 through fiscal year end and to report quarterly to the Board on realized savings.

5. Other business. None.

6. Adjournment. There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 11:14 AM.

Respectfully submitted,
S. Marchetti, Secretary
