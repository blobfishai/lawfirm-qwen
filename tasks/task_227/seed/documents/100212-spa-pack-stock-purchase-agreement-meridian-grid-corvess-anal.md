# SPA pack — Stock Purchase Agreement (Meridian Grid / Corvess Analytics) execution version

STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT — EXECUTION VERSION

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is entered into as of March 12, 2026, by and among Meridian Grid Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Buyer"), Talvane Energy Partners, LLC, a Texas limited liability company ("Seller"), and Corvess Analytics, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "Company").

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Seller owns all 2,400,000 issued and outstanding shares of common stock, par value $0.001 per share, of the Company (the "Shares"); and WHEREAS, Seller desires to sell, and Buyer desires to purchase, all of the Shares upon the terms set forth herein.

ARTICLE II — PURCHASE AND SALE

Section 2.1 Purchase Price. The aggregate consideration payable by Buyer for the Shares shall be Eighty-Four Million Dollars ($84,000,000) (the "Base Purchase Price"), payable at Closing by wire transfer of immediately available funds, subject only to the working capital adjustment described in Section 2.4.

Section 2.2 Escrow. At the Closing, Buyer shall withhold from the Base Purchase Price and deposit with Sterling Fiduciary Trust Company, N.A. (the "Escrow Agent"): (a) Six Million Three Hundred Thousand Dollars ($6,300,000) (the "Indemnity Escrow Amount"), to be held in the Indemnity Escrow Account for eighteen (18) months following the Closing Date as the primary (but not exclusive) source of recovery for indemnification claims under Article VIII; and (b) One Million Five Hundred Thousand Dollars ($1,500,000) (the "Adjustment Escrow Amount"), to be held in the Adjustment Escrow Account solely to secure the working capital adjustment under Section 2.4.

Section 2.3 No Contingent Consideration. For the avoidance of doubt, the Base Purchase Price is fixed, and no portion of the consideration payable hereunder is subject to any earn-out, milestone payment, royalty, revenue share, or other contingent payment obligation of any kind.

Section 2.4 Working Capital Adjustment. The Base Purchase Price shall be adjusted dollar-for-dollar to the extent Closing Working Capital deviates from the Target Working Capital of $9,750,000, in accordance with the procedures set forth on Exhibit C.

ARTICLE VII — CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

The obligations of Buyer to consummate the Closing are subject to: (a) the accuracy in all material respects of the representations and warranties of Seller and the Company; (b) the expiration or termination of any applicable waiting period under the HSR Act; (c) the absence of any Material Adverse Effect since the date hereof; (d) receipt of the Northgrid ISO interconnection consent; and (e) delivery of an officer's certificate, the executed Escrow Agreement, and resignations of each director of the Company.

ARTICLE VIII — INDEMNIFICATION

Section 8.1 Survival. The representations and warranties of Seller and the Company (other than the Fundamental Representations) shall survive the Closing for a period of eighteen (18) months following the Closing Date. The Fundamental Representations shall survive until sixty (60) days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. Covenants shall survive in accordance with their terms.

Section 8.2 Cap. The aggregate liability of Seller for indemnification claims under Section 8.1(a) shall not exceed Twelve Million Six Hundred Thousand Dollars ($12,600,000) (the "Cap"); provided, that the Cap shall not apply to claims arising out of the Fundamental Representations or Fraud, which shall instead be capped at the Base Purchase Price.

Section 8.3 Basket. Seller shall have no liability under Section 8.1(a) unless and until the aggregate amount of all Losses exceeds Four Hundred Twenty Thousand Dollars ($420,000) (the "Deductible"), in which case Seller shall be liable only for Losses in excess of the Deductible. The Deductible operates as a true deductible and not as a tipping or first-dollar basket.

ARTICLE X — MISCELLANEOUS

Section 10.6 Governing Law; Jurisdiction. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflicts of law principles. Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Delaware Court of Chancery (or, if such court lacks jurisdiction, the federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware).
