# SPA pack — Letter of Intent (Halden Bay / Orlyn Foods) non-binding term sheet

NON-BINDING LETTER OF INTENT

January 9, 2026

Board of Directors
Orlyn Foods Group, Inc.
41 Cortland Mill Road, Utica, New York

Re: Proposed acquisition of Orlyn Foods Group, Inc.

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter of intent (this "LOI") sets forth the principal indicative terms on which Halden Bay Capital Acquisition Corp. ("Halden Bay") proposes to acquire all of the outstanding capital stock of Orlyn Foods Group, Inc. (the "Company") from its shareholders. EXCEPT FOR THE EXCLUSIVITY AND CONFIDENTIALITY PROVISIONS IN PARAGRAPHS 7 AND 8, THIS LOI IS NON-BINDING AND DOES NOT CONSTITUTE AN OFFER, AND NO DEAL POINT BELOW IS FINAL UNTIL REFLECTED IN A DEFINITIVE EXECUTED STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT.

1. Purchase Price (indicative). An aggregate base purchase price of Fifty Million Dollars ($50,000,000), on a cash-free, debt-free basis, subject to confirmatory diligence, quality-of-earnings review, and customary working capital adjustment.

2. Escrow (indicative). An indemnity escrow equal to approximately seven percent (7%) of the base purchase price (approximately $3,500,000), held for a period to be negotiated between twelve (12) and eighteen (18) months.

3. Contingent Consideration (concept only). The parties will discuss an earn-out of up to Eight Million Dollars ($8,000,000) tied to fiscal 2027 performance metrics to be defined. Structure, thresholds, and caps remain entirely open.

4. Indemnification (indicative). Customary indemnification package with a cap in the range of 12–15% of the base purchase price and a basket in the range of 0.5% of the base purchase price; deductible-versus-tipping treatment to be negotiated.

5. Diligence and Timing. Halden Bay expects to complete diligence within 60 days, with a target signing in early April 2026.

6. Governing Documents. The definitive stock purchase agreement will supersede this LOI in all respects, and only the deal points stated in the executed agreement will govern.

7. Exclusivity. The Company grants Halden Bay exclusivity for 75 days from the date hereof.

8. Confidentiality. The parties reaffirm the mutual NDA dated November 18, 2025.

Very truly yours,
HALDEN BAY CAPITAL ACQUISITION CORP.
By: Marta Illesford, Managing Director

Agreed and acknowledged (as to Paragraphs 7 and 8 only):
ORLYN FOODS GROUP, INC.
By: Peter D. Renwick, Chief Executive Officer
