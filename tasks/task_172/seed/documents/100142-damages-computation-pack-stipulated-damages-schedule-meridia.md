# damages-computation pack — Stipulated Damages Schedule (Meridian Fabrication v. Corvess Industrial) — Final Executed Copy

STIPULATED DAMAGES SCHEDULE — FINAL EXECUTED COPY

Meridian Fabrication Corp. v. Corvess Industrial Group, LLC, No. 24-cv-8812 (D. Ariz.)

This Stipulated Damages Schedule (the "Schedule") is entered into by Plaintiff Meridian Fabrication Corp. ("Meridian") and Defendant Corvess Industrial Group, LLC ("Corvess") solely to narrow the issues for trial. This Final Executed Copy supersedes in its entirety the circulated draft designated v1, which shall have no evidentiary or computational effect.

Section 1. Definitions.
1.1 "Breach Date" means November 1, 2023, the date Corvess ceased performance under the Master Supply Agreement.
1.2 "Lost Profits" means the profits Meridian would have earned on the terminated purchase orders, as stipulated in Section 2.
1.3 "Consequential Carve-Out" means the categories of consequential damages identified in Section 5, which are excluded from Lost Profits and tracked separately on the docket.

Section 2. Stipulated Lost Profits.
2.1 The parties stipulate that Meridian's Lost Profits are fixed at exactly $1,840,000 (one million eight hundred forty thousand dollars). No further proof of the amount of Lost Profits shall be required at trial.

Section 3. Prejudgment Interest on Lost Profits.
3.1 Prejudgment interest on the Lost Profits stipulated in Section 2 shall be computed as SIMPLE interest at the rate of 6% (six percent) per annum. No compounding of any kind shall apply.
3.2 The accrual period for interest under this Section 3 is stipulated at exactly 30 (thirty) months, running from the Breach Date to the agreed judgment reference date.
3.3 For the avoidance of doubt, interest under this Section 3 applies only to the Lost Profits in Section 2 and does not apply to the Consequential Carve-Out, which is governed exclusively by the separate Prejudgment Interest Terms Memorandum.

Section 4. Total Claim for Docketing Purposes.
4.1 The total stipulated claim on the lost-profits count equals the Lost Profits in Section 2 plus the prejudgment interest computed under Section 3, rounded to whole dollars.

Section 5. Consequential Carve-Out.
5.1 The Consequential Carve-Out comprises exactly two components: (a) lost resale margin on downstream contracts, stipulated at $512,000; and (b) incidental cover costs incurred in procuring substitute fabrication capacity, stipulated at $208,000.
5.2 Prejudgment interest on the Consequential Carve-Out is NOT set by this Schedule. It is governed exclusively by the Prejudgment Interest Terms Memorandum executed by the parties, which states the applicable rate and accrual period for the carve-out.

Section 6. Rounding; No Duplication.
6.1 All computed amounts shall be rounded to whole dollars. No component of damages may be counted more than once across Sections 2 through 5.

Executed by counsel of record for Meridian and Corvess.
