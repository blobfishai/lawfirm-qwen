PREJUDGMENT INTEREST TERMS MEMORANDUM — CONSEQUENTIAL CARVE-OUT

Meridian Fabrication Corp. v. Corvess Industrial Group, LLC, No. 24-cv-8812 (D. Ariz.)

This Memorandum is the instrument referenced in Section 5.2 of the parties' Stipulated Damages Schedule (Final Executed Copy). It states the exclusive prejudgment-interest terms for the Consequential Carve-Out defined in that Schedule. It does not restate the carve-out principal, which is fixed by the components stipulated in Section 5.1 of the Schedule.

Section 1. Scope.
1.1 This Memorandum governs prejudgment interest ONLY on the Consequential Carve-Out (lost resale margin and incidental cover costs, as stipulated in the Schedule). It has no application to the Lost Profits count, whose interest terms are fixed by Section 3 of the Schedule (simple interest at 6% per annum for a stipulated 30-month period).

Section 2. Rate and Method for the Consequential Carve-Out.
2.1 Rate. Prejudgment interest on the Consequential Carve-Out accrues at the statutory rate of 8% (eight percent) per annum.
2.2 Method. Interest is SIMPLE interest. No compounding, capitalization, or interest-on-interest applies.
2.3 Accrual Period. The accrual period is stipulated at exactly 21 (twenty-one) months.
2.4 Computation. Interest equals the carve-out principal (the sum of the two components stipulated in Section 5.1 of the Schedule) multiplied by the annual rate in 2.1, prorated for the accrual period in 2.3, with the result rounded to whole dollars.

Section 3. Docketing.
3.1 The total consequential exposure to be recorded on the docket equals the carve-out principal plus the interest computed under Section 2, rounded to whole dollars.
3.2 The docket entry shall not include any Lost Profits amount or any interest computed under Section 3 of the Schedule; the lost-profits count is docketed separately.

Section 4. Miscellaneous.
4.1 This Memorandum may be amended only in a writing signed by counsel of record for both parties.
4.2 In the event of any conflict between this Memorandum and any draft schedule or prior correspondence, this Memorandum controls as to consequential-carve-out interest.

Executed by counsel of record for Meridian and Corvess as of the date of the Final Executed Copy of the Stipulated Damages Schedule.
