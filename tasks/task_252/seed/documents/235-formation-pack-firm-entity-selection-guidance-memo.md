# Formation pack — Firm entity-selection guidance memo

ENTITY SELECTION GUIDANCE (firm standard)

Step 1 — Identify the client's financing plan. Where the client anticipates institutional venture financing, the Delaware C-Corporation is the presumptive recommendation: institutional investors typically require a Delaware C-Corp for preferred-stock issuance and 83(b)/QSBS treatment.

Step 2 — Where the client anticipates NO outside institutional financing and prioritizes pass-through taxation and operational informality, the LLC is the presumptive recommendation.

Step 3 — The S-Corporation election is unavailable where the client anticipates institutional investors, because entity shareholders and multiple classes of stock are prohibited.

Step 4 — Convertible-into-corporation LLCs are disfavored by the firm: conversion at financing triggers avoidable tax and diligence cost.

Decision rule: institutional financing anticipated within 24 months => recommend delaware_c_corp, regardless of the client's stated preference for informality.
