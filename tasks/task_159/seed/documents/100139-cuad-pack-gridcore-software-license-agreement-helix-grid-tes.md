Draft circulated by counsel for Helix Grid, Inc. to Tessellate Systems Ltd. Key provisions of this draft (later REMOVED or REWRITTEN before execution):

2. License Grant (draft). Licensor grants Licensee a non-exclusive, revocable, five (5) year term license to use the Software within the Field. [Note: reversed in execution copy, which grants a perpetual, irrevocable, exclusive-in-Field license.]

6. Insurance (draft). Licensor shall maintain commercial general liability insurance of not less than $1,000,000 per occurrence and technology errors-and-omissions coverage of $3,000,000, naming Licensee as additional insured. [Note: DELETED before execution; the execution copy expressly requires no insurance from either party.]

8. Termination for Convenience (draft). Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience on thirty (30) days' written notice. [Note: DELETED before execution; the execution copy permits termination only for uncured material breach.]

9. Non-Compete (draft). Licensee shall not develop any software competitive with the Software during the term. [Note: DELETED before execution at Licensee's insistence; no non-compete survives in the execution copy.]

11. Governing Law (draft). Delaware. [Note: changed to New York in the execution copy.]

Comment log: Tessellate's markup of May 6, 2025 rejected draft Sections 6, 8, and 9 outright. Helix accepted the deletions in exchange for the covenant-not-to-sue and the four percent royalty rate. Counsel are reminded that ONLY the June 2, 2025 execution copy states the parties' operative obligations; any clause review must be run against the execution copy, not this draft.
