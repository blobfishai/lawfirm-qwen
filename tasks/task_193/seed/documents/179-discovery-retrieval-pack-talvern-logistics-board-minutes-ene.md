# Discovery-retrieval pack — Talvern Logistics board minutes: energy cost review deferred (2025-02-05)

TALVERN LOGISTICS GROUP LLC
MINUTES OF A REGULAR MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
February 5, 2025 — 10:00 AM CT — Talvern headquarters, boardroom A, and by video conference
Bates TLG-BRD-2025-02 — PRODUCED SUBJECT TO PROTECTIVE ORDER, No. 0:25-cv-01877

Present: Chair E. Talvern-Whitcomb; Directors S. Marchetti (also General Counsel and Secretary), H. Bloor, K. Osei, R. Fontaine, L. Adler, J. Whitcomb-Pryce. Also attending for portions: Chief Financial Officer N. Grieve.

1. Call to order; quorum. The Chair called the meeting to order at 10:04 AM and the Secretary confirmed a quorum.

2. Approval of prior minutes. The minutes of the December 3, 2024 regular meeting were approved as circulated.

3. Preliminary energy cost review. CFO Grieve presented a preliminary review of network energy costs, noting that fourth-quarter demand charges ran above plan and that climate-control spend at leased storage sites — including the dehumidification banks at the grain complexes — is the largest addressable category. Director Bloor asked whether any operational changes were being proposed at this time. CFO Grieve responded that management was not presenting any program or measure for approval at this meeting, that any measure touching customer-facing operations or customer contracts would be brought to the Board with a written memorandum, and that management expected to return with a defined program later in the quarter. Director Fontaine cautioned that contractual service specifications at leased sites should be reviewed before any operational change is considered. THE BOARD TOOK NO ACTION on the energy cost review and DEFERRED the topic to a future meeting pending a detailed management memorandum.

4. Insurance renewal. The Board approved the property and casualty renewal program as presented by the broker, on motion duly made and seconded, 7-0.

5. Other business. None.

6. Adjournment. The meeting adjourned at 11:02 AM.

Respectfully submitted,
S. Marchetti, Secretary
