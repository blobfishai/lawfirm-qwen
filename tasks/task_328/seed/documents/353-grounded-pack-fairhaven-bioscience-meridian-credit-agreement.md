# grounded pack — Fairhaven Bioscience / Meridian Credit Agreement, operative amendment No. 2 (executed 3 March 2026)

AMENDMENT NO. 2 TO CREDIT AGREEMENT (EXECUTED 3 MARCH 2026)

Borrower: Fairhaven Bioscience, Inc. Administrative Agent: Meridian Credit Partners, LLC.
This Amendment No. 2 amends and restates in their entirety the financial covenants at Article VII of the Credit Agreement dated 11 January 2024. Amendment No. 1 is superseded in full as to Article VII.

Section 7.1 Maximum Total Net Leverage Ratio. The Borrower shall not permit the Total Net Leverage Ratio as of the last day of any fiscal quarter to exceed 3.75:1.00.

Section 7.2 Minimum Interest Coverage Ratio. The Borrower shall not permit the Interest Coverage Ratio as of the last day of any fiscal quarter to be less than 2.50:1.00.

Section 7.3 Minimum Liquidity. The Borrower shall maintain Unrestricted Cash of not less than $18,000,000 at all times.

Section 7.4 Compliance Certificate. The Borrower shall deliver a Compliance Certificate to the Administrative Agent within 45 days after the end of each fiscal quarter, signed by a Responsible Officer, setting out the calculation of each ratio in Sections 7.1 through 7.3.

Section 7.5 Equity Cure. The Borrower may cure a breach of Section 7.1 or 7.2 no more than two (2) times in any period of four consecutive fiscal quarters, and no more than four (4) times over the life of the facility.
