# Cite pack — Cite-check policy

CITE-CHECK POLICY

Every authority in a filing must be checked against the verified citation registry before filing.

A citation is UNSAFE and must be pulled from the brief if EITHER (a) it does not appear in the registry at all, OR (b) it appears in the registry but is superseded for the proposition it is cited for.

Record the audit outcome as a count of unsafe citations. Do not attempt to verify an absent citation from memory — an unlocatable citation is unsafe by definition.
