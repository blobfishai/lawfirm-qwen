# Discovery-retrieval pack — Veritas Row Revised SOW No. 2: Talvern custodian ESI processing and production (2025-07-09)

VERITAS ROW DISCOVERY SERVICES LLC
REVISED STATEMENT OF WORK No. 2
July 9, 2025

Client: Harwick & Sloane LLP, on behalf of Meridian Grain Cooperative, Inc.
Matter: Meridian Grain Cooperative, Inc. v. Talvern Logistics Group LLC, No. 0:25-cv-01877
Subject: Collection, processing, hosted review and production of Talvern custodian ESI

1. Supersession. This Revised Statement of Work No. 2 supersedes in its entirety the initial cost estimate issued by Veritas Row on June 25, 2025, which was prepared before custodian interviews were complete and is of no further effect. Pricing in this Revised SOW No. 2 is firm for the scope stated below.

2. Scope assumptions (as revised after custodian interviews conducted July 1-3, 2025):
   (a) Nine custodians (increased from the seven assumed in the initial estimate), including the operations, energy-desk and site-management custodians identified in the parties' ESI protocol.
   (b) Collected volume of 412 GB pre-processing (increased from 300 GB assumed).
   (c) Estimated review population after de-duplication, de-NISTing and date filtering: 61,000 documents.
   (d) Rolling productions in three waves, load files per the ESI protocol, with privilege log support.

3. Pricing.
   Line 1 — Forensic collection and chain-of-custody documentation, nine custodians: 58,300 dollars.
   Line 2 — Processing, promotion and hosted review platform, nine months of hosting: 121,400 dollars.
   Line 3 — Managed review, estimated 61,000 documents at the blended rate, including quality-control sampling and privilege screening: 196,800 dollars.
   Line 4 — Production, imaging, endorsement and load-file QC across three waves: 36,000 dollars.
   TOTAL PRODUCTION COST, Lines 1 through 4: 412,500 dollars.

4. Terms. Fees on Lines 1, 2 and 4 are fixed. Line 3 is capped at the stated figure absent a written change order signed by counsel. Invoicing monthly in arrears; payment net 30. This SOW incorporates the master services agreement between Veritas Row and Harwick & Sloane dated January 9, 2024.
