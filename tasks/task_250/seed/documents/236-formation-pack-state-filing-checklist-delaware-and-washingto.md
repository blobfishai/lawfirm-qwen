# Formation pack — State filing checklist: Delaware and Washington

FORMATION FILING CHECKLIST

Delaware (entity domicile):
  1. Name availability search with the Division of Corporations.
  2. Certificate of Incorporation — filing fee $89 minimum.
  3. Registered agent appointment (annual fee).
  4. Annual franchise tax report due March 1.

Washington (foreign qualification, operations state):
  5. Foreign registration statement — filing fee $180.
  6. Business license application (UBI number).
  7. State employer registration if hiring.

Organizational items (post-filing):
  8. Initial board consent and bylaws.
  9. Founder stock issuance with vesting and 83(b) elections filed within 30 DAYS of issuance.
  10. EIN application (federal).

Total state filing fees for the Delaware + Washington pair: $269.
