# Settlement pack — Client settlement authority letter

SETTLEMENT AUTHORITY — Meridian Grain Cooperative

The Board authorises outside counsel to settle the Talvern matter for a total cash payment TO the Cooperative of not less than $1,900,000, and further authorises counsel to accept a structured settlement only if the present value is not less than that figure.

Counsel has NO authority to accept any offer below $1,900,000 without a further Board resolution. This authority expires 2026-12-31.
