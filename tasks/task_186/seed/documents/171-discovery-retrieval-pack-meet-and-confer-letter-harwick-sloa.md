# Discovery-retrieval pack — Meet-and-confer letter: Harwick & Sloane to Dunmore Pell re revised production budget (2025-07-14)

HARWICK & SLOANE LLP
Attorneys at Law — Minneapolis
July 14, 2025

BY EMAIL
Jonas Pell, Esq.
Dunmore Pell LLP
Counsel for Talvern Logistics Group LLC

Re: Meridian Grain Cooperative, Inc. v. Talvern Logistics Group LLC, No. 0:25-cv-01877 — meet and confer of July 11, 2025; Talvern custodian ESI production costs

Dear Mr. Pell:

This letter memorializes the parties' July 11 meet and confer under the Court's ESI protocol and Rule 26(f), concerning the cost of collecting, processing, reviewing and producing the Talvern custodian ESI hosted by our vendor, Veritas Row Discovery Services.

First, the parties agreed that the operative statement of the total production cost for the Talvern custodian ESI is the figure set out in Veritas Row's Revised Statement of Work No. 2 dated July 9, 2025: 412,500 dollars, covering nine custodians and three production waves. As we discussed, the vendor's earlier estimate of late June was prepared before custodian interviews, assumed a smaller custodian count and collection volume, and has been superseded; neither party will rely on it for any purpose, including cost-shifting argument.

Second, on allocation: without prejudice to either party's position on ultimate taxation of costs, the parties agreed to proceed on a 60/40 split of the total stated above (Meridian 60 percent, Talvern 40 percent), subject to reallocation by the Court on motion after substantial completion of the production waves.

Third, scheduling: wave one substantial completion by August 15, 2025; waves two and three at thirty-day intervals thereafter; privilege logs to follow each wave within twenty-one days.

If this letter does not accurately record your understanding of the July 11 conference, please advise in writing within seven days; otherwise we will treat the above as the parties' agreement and will so represent to the Court in the joint status report.

Very truly yours,
Adaeze Reyes
Partner, Harwick & Sloane LLP
Counsel for Meridian Grain Cooperative, Inc.
