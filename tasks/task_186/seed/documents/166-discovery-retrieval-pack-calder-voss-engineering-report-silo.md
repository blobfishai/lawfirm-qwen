# Discovery-retrieval pack — Calder & Voss engineering report: Silo Complex 4 data-logger analysis (2025-06-18)

CALDER & VOSS CONSULTING ENGINEERS, PLLC
Forensic Mechanical Systems Practice — Report No. CV-2025-0611
June 18, 2025

Prepared for: Harwick & Sloane LLP, counsel for Meridian Grain Cooperative, Inc.
Re: Meridian Grain Cooperative, Inc. v. Talvern Logistics Group LLC, No. 0:25-cv-01877 — continuity of dehumidification outage, Silo Complex 4, Halden Junction

1. Assignment. Counsel asked this firm to determine, from physical and electronic records, whether the dehumidification bank serving Silo Complex 4 (units DH-4A through DH-4D) was out of service continuously during the spring 2025 period, or only intermittently (for example, on an overnight-only schedule). Counsel emphasized that the produced custodian correspondence describes an overnight load-shed schedule and contains post-hoc speculation, but does not itself establish continuity of the outage.

2. Materials examined. (a) Twelve onboard interval data loggers recovered from the gallery sensor network under the parties' inspection protocol of May 29, 2025; (b) non-volatile memory images from the DH-4 motor-control-center controllers; (c) utility interval billing data for meter 771-HJ-04; (d) the Complex 4 site diary.

3. Findings.
3.1 Controller NVRAM for all four units records a de-energization event at 23:10 CT on March 14, 2025, and no subsequent re-energization event until 06:40 CT on April 20, 2025. There are no intervening start commands, manual or scheduled, in any of the four controller logs.
3.2 The gallery logger series corroborate the controller record: interior relative humidity rises monotonically from March 15, crossing the 55 percent contractual ceiling on March 17, and remains above that ceiling for 892 consecutive logged hours, with a period mean of 71 percent RH and a peak of 84 percent RH on April 11.
3.3 Utility interval data show the site's demand profile dropping by an amount consistent with the full dehumidification bank being offline continuously, not on a 22:00-06:00 cycle. An overnight-only shed would present as a diurnal sawtooth; none is present after March 14.
3.4 The site diary entries describing 'routine maintenance — air handling' are inconsistent with the controller record, which shows no maintenance lockouts, test starts, or service cycling during the period.

4. Opinion. To a reasonable degree of engineering certainty, units DH-4A through DH-4D were continuously de-energized from 23:10 CT on March 14, 2025 through 06:40 CT on April 20, 2025, and gallery humidity exceeded the 55 percent ceiling continuously from March 17 through April 20. The continuity of the outage is established by the instrument record analyzed here; it cannot be established from the correspondence alone.
