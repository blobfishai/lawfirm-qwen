# Deposition pack — Scheduling order excerpt: discovery cutoff

SCHEDULING ORDER (excerpt)

2. FACT DISCOVERY. All fact discovery, INCLUDING depositions, shall be COMPLETED by FRIDAY, OCTOBER 30, 2026. 'Completed' means the deposition has been taken, not merely noticed.

2.1 A deposition notice served on a party must be served at least 14 DAYS before the deposition date.

2.2 A non-party witness may be compelled only by SUBPOENA under Rule 45; a notice alone has no effect on a non-party.

2.3 No deposition may be scheduled after the fact-discovery cutoff absent a stipulation approved by the Court.
