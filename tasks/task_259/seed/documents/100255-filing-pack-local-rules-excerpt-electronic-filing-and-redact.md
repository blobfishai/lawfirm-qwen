# Filing pack — Local Rules excerpt: electronic filing and redaction

LOCAL RULES (excerpt)

LR 5.2 REDACTION. A party filing any document containing the following must redact: full social-security numbers (leave last four digits), full birth dates (leave year), minors' names (leave initials), and full financial-account numbers (leave last four digits). The filer, not the Clerk, bears responsibility for redaction.

LR 7.1 PAGE LIMITS. A brief in support of or in opposition to a dispositive motion shall not exceed 25 PAGES exclusive of caption, signature block, and certificate of service. Leave to exceed must be sought before filing.

LR 5.4 FILING WINDOW. A document is timely if filed by 11:59 p.m. local time on the due date. A document filed after that time is docketed as of the NEXT BUSINESS DAY.
