# Clause library — CL-117 Audit Rights

PRECEDENT CLAUSE CL-117
Category: Audit Rights
Library slug: audit_rights
Approved by: Contracts KM Committee

AUDIT RIGHTS. Customer may, not more than once per calendar year and upon thirty (30) days' notice, audit Supplier's records relating to fees charged under this Agreement. If an audit reveals an overcharge exceeding five percent (5%), Supplier shall bear the cost of the audit.

Use note: this is firm-approved precedent language; deviations require playbook escalation.
