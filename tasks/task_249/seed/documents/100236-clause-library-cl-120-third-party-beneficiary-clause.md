# Clause library — CL-120 Third party beneficiary clause

PRECEDENT CLAUSE CL-120
Category: Third party beneficiary clause
Library slug: third_party_beneficiary
Approved by: Contracts KM Committee

THIRD PARTY BENEFICIARIES. Except for the Indemnified Parties expressly identified in Section 9, this Agreement is for the sole benefit of the parties and their permitted assigns, and nothing herein confers any right or remedy on any other person.

Use note: this is firm-approved precedent language; deviations require playbook escalation.
