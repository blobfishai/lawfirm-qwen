# Clause library — CL-116 Most Favored Nation Clause

PRECEDENT CLAUSE CL-116
Category: Most Favored Nation Clause
Library slug: mfn
Approved by: Contracts KM Committee

MOST FAVORED NATION. Supplier represents that the pricing provided to Customer hereunder is no less favorable than pricing offered to any similarly situated customer purchasing comparable volumes, and shall promptly extend any more favorable pricing to Customer.

Use note: this is firm-approved precedent language; deviations require playbook escalation.
