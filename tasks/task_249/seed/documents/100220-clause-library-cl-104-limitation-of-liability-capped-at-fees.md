# Clause library — CL-104 Limitation of Liability capped at fees paid

PRECEDENT CLAUSE CL-104
Category: Limitation of Liability capped at fees paid
Library slug: limitation_of_liability_capped
Approved by: Contracts KM Committee

LIMITATION OF LIABILITY. Except for the Excluded Claims, each party's aggregate liability arising out of or related to this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid or payable by Customer to Supplier in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim. IN NO EVENT SHALL EITHER PARTY BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, SPECIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES.

Use note: this is firm-approved precedent language; deviations require playbook escalation.
