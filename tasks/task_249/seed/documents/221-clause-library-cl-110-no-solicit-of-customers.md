# Clause library — CL-110 No-Solicit of Customers

PRECEDENT CLAUSE CL-110
Category: No-Solicit of Customers
Library slug: no_solicit_customers
Approved by: Contracts KM Committee

NON-SOLICITATION. For eighteen (18) months following termination, neither party shall solicit or induce any customer of the other party with whom it had material contact during the Term to terminate or reduce its relationship with the other party.

Use note: this is firm-approved precedent language; deviations require playbook escalation.
