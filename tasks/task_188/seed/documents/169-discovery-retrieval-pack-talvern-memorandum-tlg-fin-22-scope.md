# Discovery-retrieval pack — Talvern memorandum TLG-FIN-22: scope of Cost Containment Initiative CC-7 (2025-03-28)

TALVERN LOGISTICS GROUP LLC — MANAGEMENT MEMORANDUM
No. TLG-FIN-22 — March 28, 2025
From: N. Grieve, Chief Financial Officer
To: The Board of Directors
Re: Definition and scope of Cost Containment Initiative CC-7
Bates TLG-FIN-0022 — PRODUCED SUBJECT TO PROTECTIVE ORDER, No. 0:25-cv-01877

Directors —

This memorandum defines the cost containment program that management has designated Initiative CC-7 and that will be presented for Board adoption at the April 2, 2025 meeting. Management began implementing certain CC-7 measures in mid-March under existing operating authority; Board adoption will ratify those steps and authorize continuation.

Scope of Initiative CC-7. The initiative comprises four measures:

(i) Fleet idle-reduction. Governor reprogramming and idle-shutdown timers across the linehaul fleet. Projected annualized savings: 1.9 million dollars.

(ii) Lease consolidation. Non-renewal of two third-party cross-dock leases (Cedar Falls and Roseau) at term. Projected annualized savings: 1.1 million dollars.

(iii) Suspension of active climate-control operations at leased grain-storage sites, including Silo Complex 4 at Halden Junction operated under the Meridian Warehousing and Climate Services Agreement. The dehumidification bank at Complex 4 is the largest single contributor to regional demand charges. Operations has placed the Complex 4 units on an extended shed beginning March 14, 2025. Projected savings through the shoulder season: 640,000 dollars. Management notes for completeness that the Meridian agreement contains climate specifications; operations assesses the commercial exposure as manageable relative to the projected savings.

(iv) Deferral of non-safety maintenance. Deferral of cosmetic and non-safety facility maintenance across the network to fiscal 2026. Projected savings: 700,000 dollars.

Governance. Measures (i), (ii) and (iv) are within routine management authority. Measure (iii) touches a customer contract and is therefore included in the resolution presented for adoption, so that the Board's action covers the program as a whole, including steps already taken.

N. Grieve
Chief Financial Officer
