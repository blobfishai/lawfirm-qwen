# Discovery-retrieval pack — Veritas Row initial cost estimate, Talvern custodian collection (2025-06-25, superseded)

VERITAS ROW DISCOVERY SERVICES LLC
INITIAL COST ESTIMATE — NON-BINDING — MARKED SUPERSEDED BY REVISED SOW No. 2 OF JULY 9, 2025
June 25, 2025

Client: Harwick & Sloane LLP, on behalf of Meridian Grain Cooperative, Inc.
Matter: Meridian Grain Cooperative, Inc. v. Talvern Logistics Group LLC, No. 0:25-cv-01877
Subject: Preliminary budget for collection, processing, review and production of Talvern custodian ESI

IMPORTANT: This document is a preliminary, non-binding estimate prepared before custodian interviews. It has been SUPERSEDED and should not be used for budgeting, cost-shifting or any other purpose. It is retained in the matter file for completeness only.

1. Preliminary scope assumptions. Seven custodians; estimated collected volume of 300 GB pre-processing; estimated review population of 44,000 documents after culling; two production waves.

2. Preliminary pricing (non-binding).
   Line 1 — Forensic collection, seven custodians: 46,900 dollars.
   Line 2 — Processing and hosted review platform, seven months of hosting: 98,600 dollars.
   Line 3 — Managed review, estimated 44,000 documents at the blended rate: 203,500 dollars.
   Line 4 — Production and load-file QC across two waves: 28,000 dollars.
   PRELIMINARY TOTAL: 377,000 dollars.

3. Qualifications. Pricing above assumes custodian counts and volumes provided by counsel prior to interviews. Veritas Row's experience is that custodian interviews in operational disputes of this kind commonly identify additional custodians (site-level and energy-desk personnel) and larger collections; any such change will alter Lines 1 through 4 and the total materially. A firm statement of work will follow the interview process, and only that statement of work will govern.

4. Status note added July 9, 2025 by D. Ellum: Interviews conducted July 1-3, 2025 confirmed nine custodians and 412 GB. This estimate is superseded in its entirety by Revised Statement of Work No. 2 dated July 9, 2025. Do not cite this document for the production cost.
