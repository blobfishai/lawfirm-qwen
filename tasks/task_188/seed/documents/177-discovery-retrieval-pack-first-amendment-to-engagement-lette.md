# Discovery-retrieval pack — First amendment to engagement letter: Meridian litigation fee budget (2025-06-02)

FIRST AMENDMENT TO ENGAGEMENT LETTER
June 2, 2025

This First Amendment amends the engagement letter dated August 30, 2024 (the 'Engagement Letter') between HARWICK & SLOANE LLP (the 'Firm') and MERIDIAN GRAIN COOPERATIVE, INC. (the 'Client') with respect to the matter styled Meridian Grain Cooperative, Inc. v. Talvern Logistics Group LLC, No. 0:25-cv-01877 (the 'Litigation').

Recitals. The Engagement Letter was executed when the dispute was in its pre-suit posture and set a litigation fee budget of 1,600,000 dollars through trial. Since execution, the scope of the Litigation has expanded materially: the parties' agreed ESI protocol added Talvern custodians and a vendor-hosted review; the Client has retained a forensic engineering expert; and counsel anticipates spoliation motion practice concerning the timing of Talvern's preservation efforts. The parties therefore agree to revise the budget and related terms as stated below. The original 1,600,000 dollar budget figure in Section 2 of the Engagement Letter is superseded in its entirety by this Amendment and is of no further effect.

1. Revised fee budget. Section 2 of the Engagement Letter is amended to state: 'The Firm's litigation fee budget for the Litigation, covering all phases through entry of judgment after trial but excluding any appeal, is 1,975,000 dollars. The Firm will notify the Client in writing when sixty percent and eighty-five percent of the budget have been incurred, and will not exceed the budget without the Client's prior written consent.'

2. Staffing. The core team remains A. Reyes (lead partner), two associates and one discovery counsel; the parties approve the addition of one senior associate for expert and motion practice at the rates in Schedule A, as revised.

3. Expenses. Vendor charges of Veritas Row Discovery Services and the fees of Calder & Voss Consulting Engineers are Client expenses outside the fee budget and will be billed as disbursements at cost.

4. No other changes. Except as amended above, the Engagement Letter remains in full force and effect. This Amendment is effective as of the date first written above upon execution by both parties, and is subject to ratification by the Client's board of directors at its next meeting.

HARWICK & SLOANE LLP — Adaeze Reyes, Partner
MERIDIAN GRAIN COOPERATIVE, INC. — A. Sundstrom, President
