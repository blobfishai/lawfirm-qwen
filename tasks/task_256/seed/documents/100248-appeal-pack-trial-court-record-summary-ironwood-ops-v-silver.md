# Appeal pack — Trial court record summary: Ironwood Ops v. Silverline Data

RECORD SUMMARY FOR APPEAL — Ironwood Ops v. Silverline Data

Procedural history: The trial court granted summary judgment for Silverline on Ironwood's breach-of-contract claim, holding that the limitation-of-liability clause barred the consequential damages sought.

Preserved issues: Ironwood objected to the clause's application at summary judgment and raised the gross-negligence carve-out in its opposition brief (ECF 44 at 12-14). The objection is preserved.

Record facts (undisputed below): the contract's limitation clause contains an express carve-out for gross negligence. Ironwood pleaded and supported with declaration testimony that Silverline disabled monitoring alarms for 31 days despite three written warnings. The trial court did not address the carve-out in its order.
