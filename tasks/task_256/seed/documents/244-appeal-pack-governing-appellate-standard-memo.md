# Appeal pack — Governing appellate standard memo

APPELLATE STANDARD (firm memo)

Summary judgment is reviewed DE NOVO. The appellate court applies the same standard as the trial court and views the record in the light most favorable to the non-moving party.

Disposition rule used by this firm for appeal assessment:
  - REVERSE where the trial court failed to address a preserved, potentially dispositive issue that, on the record viewed most favorably to the appellant, could defeat summary judgment.
  - AFFIRM where every preserved issue was addressed and the record admits no reasonable dispute.
  - Where the issue was NOT preserved below, the standard is plain error and the presumption is AFFIRM.

Disposition vocabulary (use exactly): reverse | affirm
