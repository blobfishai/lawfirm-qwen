Transmittal note. Quellwood colleagues — our counterproposal to your licensor form v1 is summarized below. Given board timing on our side, we would appreciate your confirming acceptance by returning a short note filed as quellwood-meridian-license-acceptance-note.docx; a full conformed draft can follow. Our positions:

1. Section 1 (Field). v1: ophthalmic optical coherence tomography imaging only. Counterproposal: the Field is expanded to all medical imaging applications. Rationale: our platform roadmap spans dermatology and cardiology probes.

2. Section 2.2 (Sublicensing). v1: no sublicenses without prior written consent in Licensor's discretion. Counterproposal: Licensee may sublicense to its Affiliates without consent, and to third-party contract manufacturers on notice.

3. Section 3.1 (Royalty). v1: running royalty of six percent (6%) of Net Sales. Counterproposal: four percent (4%). Rationale: blended-rate benchmarking against comparable interferometry licenses.

4. Section 3.2 (Minimum Royalty). v1: minimum annual royalty of $250,000. Counterproposal: deleted in its entirety.

5. Section 3.3 (Milestone). v1: $500,000 due on first regulatory clearance. Counterproposal: deferred to first commercial sale of a Licensed Product.

6. Section 5 (Audit). Accepted as drafted.

We believe these positions reflect the economic reality of a development-stage field and look forward to closing this out at the principals' call. — Meridian Optics GmbH, Legal Department
