# Hallucination-traps pack — Matter intake memorandum (Meridian Structural Claims) with open governing-law and fee-budget items

MEMORANDUM — MATTER INTAKE AND OPEN ISSUES

Matter: Meridian Structural Claims — Meridian Fabrication Group, Inc. ("Meridian") adverse to Corestone Erectors LLC ("Corestone")
From: Intake Committee
Re: New engagement acceptance; open determinations at intake

1. ENGAGEMENT SUMMARY. Meridian seeks recovery from Corestone for defective weld inspection on the Halvern Riverworks project. The principal theory is a pass-through indemnity claim: Meridian asserts that the indemnity clause in the Corestone subcontract passes through the owner's delay damages to Corestone notwithstanding the mutual waiver of consequential damages in the prime contract. Meridian estimates exposure recovery in the low seven figures.

2. DEFINITIONS. "Pass-Through Indemnity Question" means whether the subcontract indemnity clause, as drafted, reaches owner delay damages that the prime contract's consequential-damages waiver would otherwise bar. "Controlling Authority" means a decision of the supreme court of the governing jurisdiction, or an intermediate appellate decision not in conflict with any other, squarely deciding the Pass-Through Indemnity Question.

3. FACTUAL POSTURE. The subcontract was negotiated by exchange of drafts between Meridian's office in the State of Aldona and Corestone's office in the Commonwealth of Verrick. The final execution copy in our possession is signature-complete but the choice-of-law rider referenced in Section 14.2 ("Exhibit L") is not attached to any copy we have located.

4. GOVERNING LAW — STATUS: OPEN / UNRESOLVED. Two candidate jurisdictions remain: Aldona (place of Meridian's performance) and Verrick (Corestone's principal place of business and the place of final signature). Because Exhibit L is missing, the contractual choice-of-law selection cannot be confirmed from the record. Research to date (see the separate citation memorandum for this matter) has located NO Controlling Authority resolving the Pass-Through Indemnity Question under either candidate jurisdiction. As of the date of this memorandum the governing-law determination REMAINS UNRESOLVED, and no attorney should record it as settled on the present record.
