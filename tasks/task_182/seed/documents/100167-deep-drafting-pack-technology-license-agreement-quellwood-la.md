TECHNOLOGY LICENSE AGREEMENT — QUELLWOOD LABS LLC LICENSOR FORM v1

Parties. Quellwood Labs LLC, a Massachusetts limited liability company ("Licensor"), and Meridian Optics GmbH, a German limited liability company ("Licensee").

Section 1. Definitions. "Licensed Technology" means the Quellwood swept-source interferometry patents listed on Exhibit A and associated know-how. "Field" means ophthalmic optical coherence tomography imaging, and no other application. "Net Sales" means gross amounts invoiced for Licensed Products less customary deductions per Exhibit B.

Section 2. Grant. 2.1 Licensor grants Licensee an exclusive, non-transferable license under the Licensed Technology to make, have made, and sell Licensed Products solely within the Field. 2.2 No sublicensing. Licensee shall not grant sublicenses of any kind without Licensor's prior written consent, which Licensor may withhold in its discretion.

Section 3. Financial Terms. 3.1 Royalty. Licensee shall pay a running royalty of six percent (6%) of Net Sales, payable quarterly within forty-five (45) days after quarter end. 3.2 Minimum Royalty. Licensee shall pay a minimum annual royalty of $250,000, creditable against running royalties for the same year. 3.3 Milestone. Licensee shall pay $500,000 within thirty (30) days after the first regulatory clearance (FDA 510(k) or CE mark) of a Licensed Product.

Section 4. Term. Eight (8) years from the Effective Date, unless earlier terminated under Section 9.

Section 5. Records and Audit. Licensee shall keep complete records of Net Sales for five (5) years and Licensor may audit once per year on thirty (30) days' notice; underpayments above 5% carry the audit cost.

Section 9. Termination. Licensor may terminate for Licensee's material breach not cured within thirty (30) days of notice, and immediately upon Licensee's insolvency or any unauthorized sublicense.

Exhibit B (excerpt). Permitted Net Sales deductions: trade discounts actually granted; returns actually credited; transport and insurance separately invoiced; VAT and similar taxes. No deduction for commissions or bad debt.
