4. NEW Section 8.2(d) (Special Indemnity). Markup adds: the Sellers shall indemnify Buyer for any and all Losses arising out of or relating to the matters disclosed on Schedule 3.14, which indemnity shall NOT be subject to the Deductible or the cap in Section 8.3 and shall survive until the expiration of the applicable statutes of limitation.

5. Section 8.5 (Order of Recovery). Conforming change: special-indemnity claims recoverable directly from the Sellers without first exhausting the Escrow Amount.
