I am writing as partner at Fairgrove & Saldívar LLP, representing Dr. Mariana Chen-Velasco in connection with her potential engagement as Senior Vice President, Product Engineering — Autonomous Analytics at Verdana Technologies, Inc.

Thank you for providing the initial draft executive employment agreement and for the constructive discussions to date. We appreciate the thoughtfulness with which Verdana has approached this process.

Please find attached Dr. Chen-Velasco's counter-proposal, which reflects her marked-up revisions to Verdana's draft agreement (filename: Chen-Velasco_Counter-Proposal_to_Draft_Employment_Agreement_(05-28-2025).pdf). While many of the terms in the draft are acceptable and Dr. Chen-Velasco is prepared to move forward on the basis of those provisions, the counter-proposal identifies three primary areas where she requests modifications. I have summarized each below.

**1. Guaranteed Annual Bonus Floor (Years 1–2)**

Dr. Chen-Velasco requests a guaranteed bonus floor equal to 75% of the target annual bonus for her first two fiscal years of employment. Based on a target bonus of 55% of her $440,000 base salary ($242,000 per year), this equates to a guaranteed floor of $181,500 per year, or $363,000 over the two-year period.

As you know, Dr. Chen-Velasco currently holds a senior leadership position at Helios Data Systems, LLC, where she has meaningful near-term compensation at risk. The guaranteed floor is intended to mitigate the transition risk of joining a pre-IPO company mid-cycle and forgoing pending annual incentive payouts at her current employer. Importantly, this provision is structured to apply only during the initial ramp period (Years 1 and 2) and does not affect the fully discretionary nature of the bonus in Year 3 and beyond.

**2. Change-in-Control Severance Multiplier**

Dr. Chen-Velasco requests a change-in-control severance multiplier of 2.0× the sum of base salary plus target annual bonus. At the proposed compensation levels, this equates to 2.0 × $682,000 = $1,364,000.

It is Dr. Chen-Velasco's understanding that Verdana may be evaluating a potential IPO or strategic transaction within the next 18–24 months. In that context, robust CIC protections are essential to align incentives and retain key leadership through any such process. A 2.0× multiplier is consistent with market practice at the 75th percentile for similarly situated executives at enterprise SaaS companies in the $1B–$3B valuation range. This provision ensures Dr. Chen-Velasco is not penalized for joining at a pivotal pre-transaction moment.

**3. Change-in-Control Double-Trigger Equity Acceleration**

Dr. Chen-Velasco requests 100% acceleration of all unvested equity upon a qualifying termination following a change in control (double-trigger). The initial RSU grant of $2,200,000, subject to four-year vesting with a one-year cliff, would be covered by this provision.

Dr. Chen-Velasco holds 11 patents and brings 18 years of experience in autonomous analytics and product engineering. She would be forfeiting substantial unvested equity at Helios Data Systems to accept this role. Full double-trigger acceleration is necessary to ensure she is made whole in a CIC scenario and is consistent with protections offered by a meaningful portion of peer companies. Partial acceleration would leave her with materially less protection than her current Helios equity position provides.

**Additional Compensation Terms**

For completeness, the counter-proposal also reflects the following compensation terms, which we understand are generally aligned with Verdana's initial parameters:

- Base salary: $440,000 per year
- Target annual bonus: 55% of base salary ($242,000)
- Initial equity grant: $2,200,000 in RSUs, four-year vesting with one-year cliff
- Non-CIC severance: 12 months of base salary ($440,000)
- Post-termination restrictive covenant: 12 months (unchanged from Verdana's draft)

These are detailed in the attached redline.

It also informs the timeline I address below.
