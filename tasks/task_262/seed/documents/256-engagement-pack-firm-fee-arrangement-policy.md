# Engagement pack — Firm fee-arrangement policy

FEE ARRANGEMENT POLICY (firm standard)

Rule A. Contingency arrangements are permitted ONLY where the client stands to RECEIVE a recovery. A pure defense engagement produces no recovery to the client, so a percentage-of-savings contingency is not permitted; the firm treats it as an unenforceable and ethically improper fee in this jurisdiction.

Rule B. Where a client requests an impermissible arrangement, counsel must offer the nearest permissible alternative rather than declining the engagement: for defense matters, an hourly arrangement with a blended rate, or a flat fee per phase.

Rule C. Any engagement over $500,000 estimated fees requires a written engagement letter countersigned by the client before work begins.

Fee vocabulary (use exactly): hourly | flat_fee_per_phase | contingency
