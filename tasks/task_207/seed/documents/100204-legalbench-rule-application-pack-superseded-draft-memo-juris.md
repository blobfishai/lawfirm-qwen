# legalbench-rule-application pack — SUPERSEDED draft memo: jurisdiction thresholds (withdrawn, do not rely)

WHITLOCK & DEENE LLP — DRAFT MEMORANDUM (WITHDRAWN)
Status: SUPERSEDED. This draft was circulated for comment and formally withdrawn by the Civil Procedure Group. It is retained in the document store for records purposes only. Do not cite, apply, or rely on any statement in this draft. The operative guidance is the current firm rule memo on personal jurisdiction and diversity jurisdiction.

Draft Part A — Diversity thresholds (obsolete). This draft proposed screening diversity intakes against an amount-in-controversy threshold of $50,000, and further proposed that a demand of exactly the threshold amount should be treated as sufficient 'in the interest of client access to the federal forum.' Neither proposal was adopted. The comment process identified both as inconsistent with the governing statute, which requires that the matter in controversy exceed the current statutory sum. Associates who screened files against this draft between March and May were instructed to re-run their determinations.

Draft Part B — Personal jurisdiction (obsolete). This draft proposed a single-factor 'doing business' test under which any defendant who had ever transacted any business in the forum state, of any kind and at any time, would be subject to personal jurisdiction there for all claims. The comment process rejected this test as overbroad: it ignored the requirement that a claim arise out of or relate to the defendant's forum contacts, and it treated fortuitous or unrelated contacts as sufficient. The adopted rule instead requires purposeful contacts plus a nexus between the contacts and the claim, or domicile in the forum.

Draft Part C — Records note. Files screened under this draft carry the banner code CP-DRAFT-9. If you encounter a determination citing CP-DRAFT-9, flag it to the knowledge partner for re-screening under the operative memo. The withdrawal notice was circulated firm-wide, and the Civil Procedure Group's operative memo expressly supersedes this draft by name.

End of withdrawn draft. Retained unedited for the archive. For any live matter, consult the operative rule memoranda maintained by the practice groups, not this document.
