# Expert pack — Scheduling order excerpt: Meridian Grain v. Talvern

SCHEDULING ORDER (excerpt)

3. TRIAL. This matter is set for jury trial commencing MONDAY, MARCH 1, 2027.

4. EXPERT DISCLOSURES. Absent a stipulation approved by the Court, disclosures under Rule 26(a)(2) shall be made:
   (a) for the party bearing the burden of proof on an issue: AT LEAST 90 DAYS BEFORE TRIAL;
   (b) solely to contradict or rebut evidence on the same subject: WITHIN 30 DAYS AFTER the other party's disclosure.

5. No extension of the expert-disclosure dates will be granted absent a showing of good cause filed before the deadline.
