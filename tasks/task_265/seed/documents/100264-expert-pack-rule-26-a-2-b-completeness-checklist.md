# Expert pack — Rule 26(a)(2)(B) completeness checklist

RULE 26(a)(2)(B) DISCLOSURE COMPLETENESS CHECKLIST

A retained expert's written report MUST contain all six elements:
  1. a complete statement of all opinions and the basis and reasons for them;
  2. the facts or data considered by the witness;
  3. any exhibits used to summarize or support them;
  4. the witness's qualifications, INCLUDING a list of all publications authored in the previous 10 years;
  5. a list of all other cases in which the witness testified as an expert at trial or by deposition during the previous 4 years;
  6. a statement of the compensation to be paid.

A package missing ANY element is INCOMPLETE and must not be served. Vocabulary for the completeness determination (use exactly): complete | incomplete
