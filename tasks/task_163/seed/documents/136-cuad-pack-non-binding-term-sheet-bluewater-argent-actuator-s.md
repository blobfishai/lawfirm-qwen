# CUAD pack — Non-binding term sheet (Bluewater / Argent) actuator supply program

NON-BINDING TERM SHEET — ACTUATOR SUPPLY PROGRAM (Bluewater Components / Argent Motors)
Dated July 1, 2025 — FOR DISCUSSION PURPOSES ONLY

This term sheet summarizes preliminary positions for a proposed supply agreement for BA-7 brake actuators. Except for the confidentiality paragraph, this term sheet is NON-BINDING and creates no obligations. The definitive Supply Agreement, once executed, will alone govern.

Proposed terms (Argent draft positions):
- Volume: Argent to commit to a minimum of 40,000 units per year (carried into definitive agreement).
- Pricing: firm 24 months, then index-capped adjustments (carried into definitive agreement).
- AUDIT RIGHTS (proposed): Argent proposed a right to audit Bluewater's books, quality records, and plants twice per year with 10 days' notice. [Status note, September 12, 2025: REJECTED by Bluewater during definitive drafting. The executed Supply Agreement of September 9, 2025 expressly provides that Argent has no audit right and relies on certificates of conformance instead.]
- MOST FAVORED NATION (proposed): Argent proposed that if Bluewater sells equivalent actuators to any other OEM at a lower unit price, Argent receives that price retroactively. [Status note: DROPPED in definitive drafting; the executed Supply Agreement states that no most-favored-customer commitment is made.]
- Uncapped liability (proposed): Argent proposed uncapped supplier liability for recalls. [Status note: not adopted as drafted; the definitive agreement instead uses the liquidated-damages schedule and insurance requirements.]

Bluewater counter-positions accepted into the definitive agreement: change-of-control termination right for Argent, right of first refusal over the BA-7 line, dealer-network non-solicitation, two-year OEM non-compete, $5M product liability insurance, affiliate third-party-beneficiary language, Texas governing law.

Reminder to the deal team: clause extraction and evidence filings must be based on the executed Supply Agreement, not on this term sheet.
