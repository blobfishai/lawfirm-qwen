# Regulatory pack — AML Rulebook Chapter 14: Suspicious Activity Reporting (excerpt)

CHAPTER 14 — SUSPICIOUS ACTIVITY REPORTING

14.2.1 A Relevant Person must establish and maintain systems and controls to identify, assess, and report suspicious activity.

14.2.3 Guidance 10. Relevant Persons should comply with guidance issued by the supervising authority regarding identifying and reporting suspicious activity and transactions relating to money laundering, terrorist financing, and proliferation financing.

14.3.1 Where a Relevant Person knows or has reasonable grounds to suspect that a transaction involves the proceeds of crime, it must make an internal report to the Money Laundering Reporting Officer (MLRO) AS SOON AS PRACTICABLE and in any event within THREE (3) BUSINESS DAYS of the suspicion arising.

14.3.4 The MLRO must consider each internal report and, where the suspicion is sustained, submit an external Suspicious Activity Report to the Financial Intelligence Unit WITHIN FIVE (5) BUSINESS DAYS of receiving the internal report.

14.5.2 A Relevant Person must not disclose to the customer or any third party that a report has been or may be made (the 'tipping-off' prohibition).
