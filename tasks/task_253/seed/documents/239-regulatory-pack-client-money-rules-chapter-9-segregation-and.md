# Regulatory pack — Client Money Rules Chapter 9: Segregation and Reconciliation (excerpt)

CHAPTER 9 — CLIENT MONEY

9.2.1 An Authorised Person must hold Client Money separately from its own money in a Client Account designated as such with an Approved Bank.

9.4.2 An Authorised Person must perform a reconciliation of Client Money balances between its internal records and the statements of each Approved Bank NOT LESS FREQUENTLY THAN EVERY 25 BUSINESS DAYS.

9.4.6 Where a reconciliation identifies a shortfall, the Authorised Person must pay the shortfall into the Client Account BY THE CLOSE OF BUSINESS ON THE BUSINESS DAY FOLLOWING the day on which the reconciliation is performed.

9.6.1 An Authorised Person must notify the Regulator immediately upon becoming aware that it has failed to comply with the Client Money segregation requirements.
