# Clause library — CL-113 Termination for Convenience

PRECEDENT CLAUSE CL-113
Category: Termination for Convenience
Library slug: termination_convenience
Approved by: Contracts KM Committee

TERMINATION FOR CONVENIENCE. Customer may terminate this Agreement, in whole or in part, for its convenience upon sixty (60) days' prior written notice, whereupon Customer shall pay for Services performed through the effective date of termination.

Use note: this is firm-approved precedent language; deviations require playbook escalation.
