# Clause library — CL-112 Exclusivity bound by time and territory

PRECEDENT CLAUSE CL-112
Category: Exclusivity bound by time and territory
Library slug: exclusivity_time_territory
Approved by: Contracts KM Committee

EXCLUSIVITY. During the Initial Term, Distributor shall be the exclusive distributor of the Products within the Territory (defined as the Pacific Northwest region), and Supplier shall not appoint any other distributor within the Territory.

Use note: this is firm-approved precedent language; deviations require playbook escalation.
