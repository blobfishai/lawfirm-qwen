# CUAD pack — Master Services Agreement (Meridian Analytics / Corvus Freight) execution copy

MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (this "Agreement"), dated as of March 14, 2025, is entered into by and between Meridian Analytics LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("Provider"), and Corvus Freight Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Client"). This execution copy supersedes all prior drafts and term sheets exchanged between the parties.

1. Services; Non-Exclusive Relationship. Provider shall perform the data-engineering and freight-network analytics services described in mutually executed Statements of Work ("SOWs"). This Agreement is strictly non-exclusive: nothing herein restricts Client from engaging any other provider of similar services, or Provider from serving any other customer, including competitors of Client. Neither party makes any exclusive dealing commitment.

2. Fees. Fees are as stated in each SOW. Client has no minimum purchase, spend, or volume obligation under this Agreement. No most-favored-customer or most-favored-nation pricing commitment is made by either party.

3. Work Product; Assignment of Intellectual Property. All deliverables, reports, and models first created by Provider under an SOW ("Work Product") shall be the sole property of Client. Provider hereby irrevocably assigns to Client all right, title, and interest, including all intellectual property rights, in the Work Product, effective upon payment of the applicable fees.

4. Non-Solicitation of Personnel. During the term and for twelve (12) months after expiration or termination, neither party shall directly solicit for employment any employee or contractor of the other party who was materially involved in the Services. General public job postings are not a breach.

5. Insurance. Provider shall maintain commercial general liability insurance of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence and cyber liability insurance of not less than $5,000,000 in the aggregate, naming Client as an additional insured, with certificates delivered on request.

6. Audit Rights. Client may, on fifteen (15) days' written notice and not more than once in any twelve-month period, audit Provider's books and records related to invoiced fees and compliance with Sections 3 and 9, during normal business hours.

7. Term; Termination for Convenience. This Agreement continues until terminated. Either party may terminate this Agreement or any SOW for convenience, without cause and without penalty, upon sixty (60) days' prior written notice.

8. Post-Termination Transition Services. For ninety (90) days after any expiration or termination, Provider shall provide transition assistance, including data export in industry-standard formats and knowledge-transfer sessions, at the rates in the most recent SOW.

9. Limitation of Liability. EXCEPT AS SET FORTH IN THE NEXT SENTENCE, EACH PARTY'S AGGREGATE LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT SHALL NOT EXCEED THE FEES PAID OR PAYABLE BY CLIENT IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM. THE CAP SHALL NOT APPLY TO PROVIDER'S BREACH OF ITS CONFIDENTIALITY OBLIGATIONS OR TO PROVIDER'S INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY INFRINGEMENT INDEMNITY, AS TO WHICH PROVIDER'S LIABILITY IS UNCAPPED AND UNLIMITED. No liquidated damages or termination fees are payable under this Agreement.

10. Assignment. Neither party may assign this Agreement, in whole or in part, without the prior written consent of the other party. A change of control of a party (including by merger or sale of stock) shall NOT be deemed an assignment, shall require no consent or notice, and shall give the other party no termination right.

11. Warranty. Provider warrants only that Services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner. No warranty period of any specific duration is stated.

12. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles.
