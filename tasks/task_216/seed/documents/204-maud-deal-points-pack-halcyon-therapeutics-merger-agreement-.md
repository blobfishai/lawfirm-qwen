# MAUD deal points pack — Halcyon Therapeutics merger agreement draft No. 3 (SUPERSEDED) — prior termination-fee construct

DRAFT No. 3 — May 11, 2026 — SUPERSEDED; retained for the negotiation history file only. Do not cite for current deal points; the execution version dated June 2, 2026 controls.

Excerpt of draft Article VIII, Halcyon Therapeutics / Meridian Compass Agreement and Plan of Merger, reflecting Parent's counsel's markup before the June 1 all-hands call.

Draft Section 8.1(b): the Merger to be consummated on or before December 31, 2026 (the draft "Outside Date"), with a single three-month extension for the Regulatory Condition. [Rejected by the Company; the parties later agreed to a longer initial period.]

Draft Section 8.2: Company Termination Fee of $110,000,000, payable on fiduciary termination or following an Adverse Recommendation Change, with a fifteen (15) month tail. [The tail was shortened and the fee re-cut against the final equity value in the executed agreement.]

Draft Section 8.3: Parent Termination Fee of $240,000,000, payable only on antitrust failure. [The executed agreement broadened the trigger to include Parent's failure to close and increased the amount.]

Draft Section 5.4: matching rights of three (3) Business Days initial and two (2) Business Days for amendments. [Extended in the executed agreement.]

Negotiation note (A. Renfield, Ellsworth & Crane LLP): Company countered at 2.6% of equity value on the company-side fee; Parent's opening ask was 3.9%. The compromise reflected in the execution version was struck at the June 1 principals' call. All figures in this draft are of historical interest only and do not reflect the executed deal points.
