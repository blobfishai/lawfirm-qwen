# MAUD deal points pack — Merger Agreement (Aurelian Summit / Silverline Data Systems) execution version — tender offer terms, Offer conditions and MAE definition excerpt

CONFORMED EXCERPT — AGREEMENT AND PLAN OF MERGER, dated as of July 20, 2026, by and among Aurelian Summit Group plc, a public limited company organized under the laws of England and Wales ("Parent"), Summit Argent Merger Sub, Inc., a Delaware corporation and an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of Parent ("Purchaser"), and Silverline Data Systems, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "Company"). Excerpt of Article I (the Offer), Article VI (Conditions to the Offer) and the definition of Company Material Adverse Effect, prepared for the deal-points file by Ellsworth & Crane LLP from the EXECUTION VERSION.

Section 1.1 The Offer. Provided that this Agreement shall not have been terminated in accordance with Article VIII, Purchaser shall commence (within the meaning of Rule 14d-2 under the Exchange Act), as promptly as practicable and in any event within ten (10) Business Days after the date hereof, a tender offer (the "Offer") to purchase all of the issued and outstanding shares of Company Common Stock at a price per share of $47.25, net to the seller in cash, without interest (the "Offer Price"). This is a two-step transaction: as soon as practicable following the Acceptance Time, the Merger shall be effected without a vote of the Company's stockholders pursuant to Section 251(h) of the DGCL, and each share not accepted in the Offer (other than Excluded Shares and Dissenting Shares) shall be converted into the right to receive the Offer Price.

Section 6.1 Conditions to the Offer. Notwithstanding any other provision of the Offer, Purchaser shall not be required to accept for payment any shares of Company Common Stock validly tendered unless, at the scheduled Expiration Time: (a) the number of shares validly tendered and not validly withdrawn, together with shares owned by Parent and its Subsidiaries, represents at least a majority of the shares of Company Common Stock then outstanding (the "Minimum Condition"); (b) the waiting period under the HSR Act shall have expired or been terminated and the requisite foreign direct investment clearances obtained (the "Regulatory Condition"); (c) since the date of this Agreement there shall not have occurred a Company Material Adverse Effect that is continuing; and (d) the aggregate number of shares of Company Common Stock with respect to which the holders thereof have properly exercised and not withdrawn or lost appraisal rights under Section 262 of the DGCL (the "Dissenting Shares") shall not exceed fifteen percent (15%) of the shares of Company Common Stock outstanding immediately prior to the Expiration Time (the "Appraisal Rights Condition").

Section 8.1(b) Outside Date. Either Parent or the Company may terminate this Agreement if the Acceptance Time shall not have occurred on or before November 14, 2026 (the "Outside Date"), automatically extended to February 14, 2027 if on such date the Regulatory Condition (but no other condition) remains unsatisfied.

Definition — "Company Material Adverse Effect" (condensed). The definition follows the customary two-part structure, with carveouts for: general economic and financial market conditions; conditions generally affecting the Company's industry; changes in Law or GAAP; epidemics, pandemics and other public health events; acts of war, terrorism and natural disasters; the announcement or pendency of the transactions; and failure to meet projections. The disproportionate-impact modifier applies only to the economic-conditions, industry-conditions and war/terrorism carveouts; it does NOT apply to the change-in-Law or pandemic carveouts in this agreement.
