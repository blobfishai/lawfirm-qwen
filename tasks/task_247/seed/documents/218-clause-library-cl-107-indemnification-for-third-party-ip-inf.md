# Clause library — CL-107 Indemnification for third-party IP infringement

PRECEDENT CLAUSE CL-107
Category: Indemnification for third-party IP infringement
Library slug: indemnification_ip
Approved by: Contracts KM Committee

INDEMNIFICATION. Supplier shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Customer from and against any third-party claim alleging that the Services infringe or misappropriate any patent, copyright, trademark, or trade secret, and shall pay all damages finally awarded or amounts agreed in settlement, provided Customer gives prompt notice and reasonable cooperation.

Use note: this is firm-approved precedent language; deviations require playbook escalation.
