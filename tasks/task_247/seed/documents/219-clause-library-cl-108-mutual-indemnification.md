# Clause library — CL-108 Mutual indemnification

PRECEDENT CLAUSE CL-108
Category: Mutual indemnification
Library slug: indemnification_mutual
Approved by: Contracts KM Committee

INDEMNIFICATION. Each party shall defend the other against third-party claims arising from the indemnifying party's gross negligence, willful misconduct, or breach of applicable law, and shall indemnify the other for damages finally awarded, subject to prompt notice, sole control of defense, and reasonable cooperation.

Use note: this is firm-approved precedent language; deviations require playbook escalation.
