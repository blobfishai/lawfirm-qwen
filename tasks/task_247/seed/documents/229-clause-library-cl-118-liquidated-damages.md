# Clause library — CL-118 Liquidated Damages

PRECEDENT CLAUSE CL-118
Category: Liquidated Damages
Library slug: liquidated_damages
Approved by: Contracts KM Committee

LIQUIDATED DAMAGES. The parties agree that actual damages for delay would be difficult to determine, and Supplier shall therefore pay liquidated damages of $2,500 per day of delay beyond the Milestone Date, capped at ten percent (10%) of the Contract Value, as the parties' sole monetary remedy for delay.

Use note: this is firm-approved precedent language; deviations require playbook escalation.
