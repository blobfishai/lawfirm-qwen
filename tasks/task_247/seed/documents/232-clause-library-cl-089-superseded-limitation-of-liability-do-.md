# Clause library — CL-089 SUPERSEDED Limitation of Liability (do not use)

PRECEDENT CLAUSE CL-089 — WITHDRAWN
Category: Limitation of Liability

This clause capped liability at the fees paid in the preceding THREE months and contained no carve-outs for confidentiality or IP indemnity. It was withdrawn by the Contracts KM Committee after the 2025 playbook revision. Do not select this clause for new drafting.
