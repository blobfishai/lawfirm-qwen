# Clause library — CL-106 Limitation of Liability with a super-cap for data incidents

PRECEDENT CLAUSE CL-106
Category: Limitation of Liability with a super-cap for data incidents
Library slug: limitation_of_liability_supercap
Approved by: Contracts KM Committee

LIMITATION OF LIABILITY. Supplier's aggregate liability for claims arising from a Security Incident shall not exceed three (3) times the fees paid in the preceding twelve (12) months (the 'Super-Cap'), and all other claims shall remain subject to the General Cap.

Use note: this is firm-approved precedent language; deviations require playbook escalation.
