# Clause library — CL-105 Limitation of Liability with uncapped carve-outs

PRECEDENT CLAUSE CL-105
Category: Limitation of Liability with uncapped carve-outs
Library slug: limitation_of_liability_uncapped_carveout
Approved by: Contracts KM Committee

LIMITATION OF LIABILITY. The limitations in this Section shall not apply to (a) a party's indemnification obligations for third-party intellectual-property claims, (b) breach of confidentiality, (c) gross negligence or willful misconduct, or (d) a party's payment obligations, each of which shall be uncapped.

Use note: this is firm-approved precedent language; deviations require playbook escalation.
