TRELLIS BIOWORKS, INC. — DISCLOSURE SCHEDULES TO THE SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT
SCHEDULE 3.14 — LITIGATION AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
Capitalized terms have the meanings given in the Share Purchase Agreement. Disclosure of a matter is not an admission of liability or materiality.

Item 1. Trademark opposition (pending). TTAB Opposition No. 91-284772, Lumenor Devices B.V. v. Trellis BioWorks, Inc., opposing registration of the Company's mark TRELLIGEN (U.S. App. Serial No. 98/415,882), filed 11 February 2026. Status: pending; discovery conference held 5 May 2026. No damages are claimed in the proceeding; the exposure is potential refusal of registration and associated rebranding of the TRELLIGEN assay line. The Company continues to use the mark at common law.

Item 2. Customer dispute (pending, pre-litigation). Written demand of 3 April 2026 from Bexley Clinical Partners LLC claiming $1,850,000 in damages for alleged assay lot failures under the 2024 Supply Agreement between the Company and Bexley. Status: pending; no suit filed; the Company has denied liability in its response of 24 April 2026 and asserts the lot conformed to the released specification. Insurance carrier noticed 28 April 2026; coverage position reserved.

Item 3. OSHA citation (resolved). Citation No. 1829445 (Region IV) issued 9 January 2026 relating to chemical-hygiene documentation at the Company's Norcross facility. Resolved by informal settlement; penalty of $46,500 paid in full on 17 March 2026; abatement certified 2 March 2026. This matter is closed and is disclosed as a resolved matter only.

End of Schedule 3.14. Cross-reference: insurance policies responsive to Item 2 are listed on Schedule 3.18; the TRELLIGEN registration file is indexed on Schedule 3.13 (Intellectual Property Registrations).
