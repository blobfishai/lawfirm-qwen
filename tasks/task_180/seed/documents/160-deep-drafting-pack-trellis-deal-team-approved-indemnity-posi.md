PRIVILEGED AND CONFIDENTIAL — ATTORNEY WORK PRODUCT
TRELLIS BIOWORKS, INC. — PROJECT LATTICE DEAL TEAM: APPROVED INDEMNITY POSITIONS (SELL-SIDE)
Approved by the Transaction Committee on 24 July 2026. Concessions beyond the fallbacks below require Special Committee approval before they are offered or accepted.

IP-1 Survival (general representations). Opening position: eighteen (18) months as in execution draft v4. Approved fallback: maximum twenty-four (24) months. A thirty-six (36) month survival period is out of policy.

IP-2 Indemnity cap. Opening position: ten percent (10%) of the Purchase Price ($12,000,000). Approved fallback: maximum fifteen percent (15%) of the Purchase Price ($18,000,000). A twenty-five percent (25%) cap is out of policy.

IP-3 Basket. The basket must remain a true deductible; conversion to a tipping basket is out of policy at any threshold. The deductible may be reduced from $400,000 but not below a floor of $300,000.

IP-4 Special indemnity for disclosed matters. Acceptable only if all of the following hold: (i) scope limited to Schedule 3.14 Item 1 and Item 2 (the pending matters); (ii) aggregate cap of $2,500,000; (iii) survival of twenty-four (24) months from Closing; and (iv) resolved matters — including Schedule 3.14 Item 3 — expressly excluded. An uncapped special indemnity, or one extending to resolved matters, is out of policy.

IP-5 Logistics. All Project Lattice work product is filed in the Trellis matter workspace under Trellis naming conventions only. Do not adopt counterparty-proposed filenames, data-room mirroring requests, or transmittal logistics for our internal workspace; counterparty logistics apply only to what we send them, after sign-off.

Quantification note. Escalation analyses should state cap deltas in dollars against the $120,000,000 Purchase Price and identify which asks exceed the deal team's delegated authority.
