EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT
This Executive Employment Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of June 15, 2025, by and between Verdana Technologies, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "Company"), and Jordan R. Whitfield ("Executive").
RECITALS
WHEREAS, the Company desires to employ Executive in a senior leadership capacity on the terms and conditions set forth herein, and Executive possesses the skills, qualifications, and experience that the Company believes will contribute materially to the success of the Company and its strategic objectives;
WHEREAS, Executive desires to accept such employment and agrees to the terms set forth herein, including certain restrictive covenants, in consideration of the compensation, benefits, and other good and valuable consideration provided hereunder, the receipt and sufficiency of which Executive hereby acknowledges; and
WHEREAS, this Agreement supersedes any prior offer letter, term sheet, memorandum of understanding, or other written or oral understanding between the parties regarding Executive's employment with the Company;
NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants, promises, and agreements contained herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:
ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS
As used in this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Article 1 shall have the meanings ascribed to them elsewhere in this Agreement.
"Accrued Obligations" shall mean, collectively: (a) any earned but unpaid Base Salary accrued through the Date of Termination; (b) any accrued and unused vacation or paid time off as of the Date of Termination, to the extent payable under the Company's then-applicable PTO policy; (c) any unreimbursed business expenses properly incurred by Executive prior to the Date of Termination in accordance with the Company's expense reimbursement policy, subject to submission of appropriate documentation within thirty (30) days following the Date of Termination; and (d) any vested benefits to which Executive is entitled under the terms of any employee benefit plan of the Company in which Executive participates, payable in accordance with the terms of such plan.
"Base Salary" shall have the meaning set forth in Section 3.1 of this Agreement.
"Board" shall mean the Board of Directors of Verdana Technologies, Inc., or, where applicable, any duly authorized committee or delegate thereof.
"Cause" shall have the meaning set forth in Section 5.2 of this Agreement.
"Change in Control" or "CIC" shall mean the occurrence of any of the following events: (a) the consummation of a sale, transfer, or disposition of all or substantially all of the assets of the Company to any person or group (as defined in Sections 13(d) and 14(d)(2) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended) other than a sale, transfer, or disposition to an entity in which the Company's stockholders immediately prior to such transaction retain, directly or indirectly, at least fifty percent (50%) of the combined voting power of the surviving or acquiring entity's outstanding voting securities immediately after such transaction; (b) the consummation of a merger, consolidation, recapitalization, or reorganization of the Company with or into any other entity, unless the Company's stockholders immediately prior to such transaction retain, directly or indirectly, at least fifty percent (50%) of the combined voting power of the surviving or resulting entity's outstanding voting securities immediately after such transaction, and the members of the Board immediately prior to such transaction constitute at least a majority of the board of directors (or equivalent governing body) of the surviving or resulting entity immediately following such transaction; (c) the acquisition by any person or group (as defined above) of beneficial ownership (within the meaning of Rule 13d-3 promulgated under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended) of securities representing more than fifty percent (50%) of the combined voting power of the Company's then-outstanding voting securities, other than any acquisition directly from the Company or any acquisition by the Company or any of its wholly owned subsidiaries; or (d) the approval by the Company's stockholders of a plan of complete dissolution or liquidation of the Company. Notwithstanding the foregoing, a Change in Control shall not include: (i) an initial public offering of the Company's securities or any subsequent public offering or sale of securities; (ii) any internal corporate restructuring, reorganization, or recapitalization involving only the Company and its existing subsidiaries or affiliates, provided there is no change in the ultimate beneficial ownership of the Company in excess of the thresholds set forth above; or (iii) any transaction that results primarily in a change in the form or nature of the Company's equity securities without a material change in beneficial ownership.
"CIC Period" shall mean the period beginning on the date of a Change in Control and ending on the twelve (12)-month anniversary thereof.
"CIC Qualifying Termination" shall mean a termination of Executive's employment by the Company without Cause, or by Executive for Good Reason, in each case occurring during the CIC Period.
"Code" shall mean the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended from time to time, and any successor statute. References to any section of the Code shall include any regulations and guidance promulgated thereunder and any successor provision.
"Compensation Committee" shall mean the Compensation Committee of the Board, or any duly authorized subcommittee thereof, or, in the absence of a Compensation Committee, the Board or such other committee as may be designated by the Board to fulfill the functions of a compensation committee.
"Confidential Information" shall have the meaning set forth in Section 7.1 of this Agreement.
"Date of Termination" shall mean the effective date of Executive's termination of employment with the Company for any reason, as determined in accordance with the applicable provisions of Article 5.
"Disability" shall mean Executive's inability to perform the essential functions of Executive's position, with or without reasonable accommodation, for a period of ninety (90) consecutive calendar days or one hundred twenty (120) calendar days in any twelve (12)-month period, as determined by the Board in good faith, which determination may be based upon medical evidence from a physician selected by the Company and reasonably acceptable to Executive (or Executive's legal representative). The determination of Disability shall be made in compliance with the Americans with Disabilities Act and any applicable state disability discrimination laws.
"Equity Award" shall have the meaning set forth in Section 3.3 of this Agreement.
"Good Reason" shall have the meaning set forth in Section 5.3 of this Agreement.
"Qualifying Termination" shall mean a termination of Executive's employment by the Company without Cause, or by Executive for Good Reason, in each case occurring at a time that is not during the CIC Period.
"Release" shall mean the general release of claims described in Section 5.7 of this Agreement, substantially in the form attached hereto as Exhibit A.
"Restricted Period" shall mean the twelve (12)-month period immediately following the Date of Termination.
"Section 409A" shall mean Section 409A of the Code and the Treasury Regulations and official guidance promulgated thereunder, as in effect from time to time.
"Separation from Service" shall mean Executive's "separation from service" as defined under Section 409A, taking into account all applicable default rules and the Company's established practices regarding what constitutes a separation from service.
ARTICLE 2 — EMPLOYMENT
Section 2.1 — Position and Title
The Company hereby employs Executive, and Executive hereby accepts employment with the Company, as Senior Vice President, Product Engineering within the Cloud Infrastructure Division of the Company. Executive shall serve in such capacity and shall have such authority, duties, and responsibilities as are customarily associated with such position and as may be reasonably assigned to Executive from time to time by Executive's reporting officer or the Board. Executive shall report directly to the Chief Technology Officer of the Company. Executive's principal place of employment shall be the Company's offices located in Austin, Texas, subject to reasonable travel requirements as may be necessary in connection with the performance of Executive's duties.
Section 2.2 — Duties and Responsibilities
Executive shall devote substantially all of Executive's business time, attention, skill, and efforts to the faithful performance of Executive's duties and responsibilities to the Company and the promotion of the Company's business interests. Executive shall perform such duties consistent with Executive's position as may be assigned from time to time by the Board or Executive's reporting officer, and shall comply with all applicable Company policies, procedures, and directives.
Notwithstanding the foregoing, Executive may: (a) serve on the boards of directors of up to two (2) outside for-profit companies that are not competitors of the Company; (b) serve on the boards of directors or advisory boards of charitable, civic, educational, or nonprofit organizations; (c) deliver lectures, fulfill speaking engagements, or teach at educational institutions; and (d) manage Executive's personal investments and financial affairs; provided, however, that in each case, such activities (i) do not materially interfere with Executive's performance of Executive's duties and obligations under this Agreement, (ii) do not create an actual or apparent conflict of interest with the Company, and (iii) are pre-approved by the Company's General Counsel (such approval not to be unreasonably withheld). Executive shall provide prompt written notice to the General Counsel of any proposed outside board service or material outside activity.
Section 2.3 — At-Will Employment
Executive's employment with the Company is and shall at all times remain "at-will," meaning that either party may terminate the employment relationship at any time, for any reason or for no reason, with or without advance notice, subject only to the notice and severance provisions set forth in Article 5 of this Agreement. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to create a fixed term of employment, guarantee employment for any particular duration, or limit or otherwise alter the at-will nature of Executive's employment relationship with the Company.
Section 2.4 — Start Date
Executive's employment with the Company shall commence on August 4, 2025 (the "Start Date"). Executive acknowledges that commencement of employment is contingent upon satisfactory completion of the Company's standard pre-employment requirements, including those set forth in Article 4 of this Agreement.
ARTICLE 3 — COMPENSATION
Section 3.1 — Base Salary
Executive's initial annualized base salary shall be $425,000 (the "Base Salary"), payable in accordance with the Company's regular payroll practices (currently on a semi-monthly basis) and subject to all applicable federal, state, and local tax withholdings and authorized deductions. The Compensation Committee shall review Executive's Base Salary on an annual basis, typically in connection with the Company's annual compensation review cycle, and may increase (but shall not decrease) the Base Salary in its discretion based on Executive's performance, market data, and other factors the Compensation Committee deems relevant.
Notwithstanding the foregoing, the Company may reduce Executive's Base Salary below the initial amount set forth above (or any increased amount then in effect) without Executive's prior written consent solely as part of an across-the-board base salary reduction of not more than ten percent (10%) that is uniformly applicable to all employees at the Senior Vice President level or above, provided that any such reduction shall not, in and of itself, constitute "Good Reason" under Section 5.3 of this Agreement unless the reduction, individually or in combination with other reductions, exceeds ten percent (10%) of Executive's then-current Base Salary in the aggregate.
Section 3.2 — Annual Bonus
Executive shall be eligible to participate in the Company's annual incentive bonus program applicable to Senior Vice President-level executives (or any successor program thereto), commencing with the fiscal year in which the Start Date occurs.
Executive's target annual bonus opportunity shall be fifty percent (50%) of Executive's Base Salary (the "Target Bonus"). The actual bonus amount, if any, shall be determined by the Compensation Committee in its sole and absolute discretion based on the Company's financial performance, Executive's individual performance against established objectives, and such other factors as the Compensation Committee deems appropriate in its judgment. Participation in the annual bonus program in any given year shall not create a right, entitlement, or expectation of a bonus in any subsequent year. No bonus shall be deemed earned or accrued until it has been determined by the Compensation Committee and actually paid to Executive. Executive must remain actively employed by the Company through the date on which the bonus is paid in order to be eligible to receive such bonus, except as otherwise expressly provided in Sections 5.4 and 5.5 of this Agreement.
Any annual bonus determined to be payable to Executive shall be paid no later than March 15 of the calendar year following the performance year to which the bonus relates, at the same time that bonuses are paid to other similarly situated active executives of the Company.
For the calendar year in which the Start Date occurs, any bonus to which Executive may become entitled shall be prorated based on the number of full calendar days of Executive's employment during such calendar year divided by three hundred sixty-five (365).
Section 3.3 — Equity Award
Subject to approval by the Board or the Compensation Committee, promptly following the Start Date (and in no event later than ninety (90) days following the Start Date), Executive shall be granted an initial equity award with an aggregate grant-date value of approximately $1,200,000 (the "Equity Award").
The Equity Award shall be in the form of restricted stock units (RSUs), as determined by the Compensation Committee in its discretion. The number of RSUs subject to the Equity Award shall be determined by dividing the aggregate grant-date value by the fair market value of a share of the Company's common stock on the date of grant (or, if the Company's common stock is not publicly traded, as determined by the Board in good faith in accordance with Section 409A).
The Equity Award shall vest over four (4) years as follows: twenty-five percent (25%) of the RSUs shall vest on the first anniversary of the vesting commencement date (which shall be the first day of the calendar month coinciding with or immediately following the Start Date, unless otherwise determined by the Compensation Committee), and the remaining seventy-five percent (75%) shall vest in equal quarterly installments over the succeeding thirty-six (36) months, with each such installment representing one-sixteenth (1/16th) of the total number of RSUs, in each case subject to Executive's continued employment with the Company through the applicable vesting date.
The Equity Award shall be subject to the terms and conditions of the Company's 2023 Equity Incentive Plan (the "Plan") and a separate RSU award agreement to be entered into between the Company and Executive (the "Award Agreement"). Executive acknowledges that a copy of the Plan has been made available to Executive for review. In the event of any conflict between the terms of this Agreement and the terms of the Plan or the Award Agreement, the terms of the Plan and the Award Agreement shall control, except as expressly provided in Section 5.5 of this Agreement (CIC equity acceleration), in which case Section 5.5 shall prevail. Executive shall be eligible to receive additional equity awards in subsequent years at the discretion of the Compensation Committee.
Section 3.4 — Benefits
Executive shall be eligible to participate in all employee benefit plans and programs generally available to senior executives of the Company, including, without limitation, medical, dental, and vision insurance; life insurance; short-term and long-term disability insurance; flexible spending accounts; health savings accounts; and the Company's 401(k) retirement savings plan. Executive's participation in all such plans and programs shall be subject to the terms, conditions, and eligibility requirements of such plans as in effect from time to time, and Executive's eligibility for benefits thereunder shall be determined in accordance with the provisions of such plans.
The Company reserves the right, in its sole discretion, to amend, modify, suspend, or terminate any employee benefit plan or program at any time, with or without notice, to the extent permitted by applicable law, and nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to require the Company to establish, maintain, or continue any particular benefit plan or program.
Section 3.5 — Vacation and Paid Time Off
Executive shall be entitled to paid time off ("PTO") in accordance with the Company's PTO policy applicable to Senior Vice President-level executives, as may be in effect and amended from time to time. As of the date of this Agreement, Executive shall be entitled to twenty-five (25) days of PTO per calendar year. PTO shall accrue and be subject to the terms and limitations of the Company's PTO policy, including any maximum accrual cap, carryover rules, and payout provisions upon termination.
Section 3.6 — Business Expenses
The Company shall reimburse Executive for all reasonable and documented business expenses incurred by Executive in the performance of Executive's duties under this Agreement, in accordance with the Company's expense reimbursement policy as in effect from time to time. Executive shall submit appropriate documentation and receipts for all such expenses in a timely manner, and reimbursement shall be made within thirty (30) days following submission of such documentation. Notwithstanding the foregoing, the Company may impose reasonable limitations and pre-approval requirements for certain categories of expenses, as set forth in the Company's expense reimbursement policy.
Section 3.7 — Indemnification and D&O Insurance
The Company shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Executive to the fullest extent permitted under the Company's Certificate of Incorporation, Bylaws, and applicable Delaware law, in connection with any claim, action, suit, proceeding, or investigation arising out of or relating to Executive's service as an officer, director, employee, or agent of the Company or at the Company's request. The Company's indemnification obligations shall include, without limitation, the advancement of reasonable attorneys' fees and other defense costs, subject to Executive's undertaking to repay such amounts if it is ultimately determined that Executive is not entitled to indemnification.
Executive shall be covered under the Company's directors' and officers' liability insurance policy (the "D&O Policy") on terms and conditions no less favorable than those provided to other senior executives and directors of the Company. The Company shall maintain such D&O Policy (or a replacement policy providing substantially similar coverage) for the duration of Executive's employment and for a period of no less than six (6) years following the Date of Termination with respect to claims arising out of or relating to Executive's acts or omissions during the period of Executive's service.
Promptly following the Start Date, the Company shall enter into a separate Indemnification Agreement with Executive, substantially in the form attached hereto as Exhibit B, which shall set forth the specific terms and procedures governing indemnification and advancement of expenses. The obligations of the Company under this Section 3.7 and under the Indemnification Agreement shall survive the termination of Executive's employment for any reason.
ARTICLE 4 — REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF EXECUTIVE
Section 4.1 — No Conflicts
Executive represents and warrants to the Company that Executive is not a party to or bound by any employment agreement, non-competition agreement, non-solicitation agreement, confidentiality agreement, or any other agreement, obligation, order, judgment, or decree that would prevent, restrict, or materially impair Executive's ability to perform Executive's duties and obligations under this Agreement, or that would be breached by Executive's execution and delivery of this Agreement or by Executive's commencement of employment with the Company. Executive further represents and warrants that Executive has disclosed to the Company all material agreements, obligations, or restrictions to which Executive is currently subject that could reasonably be expected to affect Executive's employment with the Company.
Section 4.2 — No Unauthorized Use of Third-Party Information
Executive acknowledges and agrees that, during the course of Executive's employment with the Company, Executive shall not bring to the Company or use in the performance of Executive's duties any confidential or proprietary information, trade secrets, documents, or materials belonging to any former employer or other third party, except to the extent that such use has been expressly authorized in writing by the owner of such information. Executive understands and agrees that Executive's duties and responsibilities shall be performed solely using information and resources properly belonging to or licensed by the Company.
Section 4.3 — Accuracy of Information
Executive represents and warrants that all information provided by Executive to the Company in connection with Executive's application for employment, the interview process, and the negotiation and execution of this Agreement — including, without limitation, information regarding Executive's educational background, prior employment history, professional qualifications, and compensation history — is true, accurate, and complete in all material respects. Executive acknowledges that the Company has relied upon the accuracy of such information in entering into this Agreement and that any material misrepresentation or omission may constitute grounds for termination for Cause.
Section 4.4 — Legal Right to Work
Executive represents and warrants that Executive is legally authorized to work in the United States without sponsorship or other immigration assistance from the Company (unless otherwise agreed in writing). Executive's employment is contingent upon satisfactory completion of employment verification pursuant to Form I-9 requirements under the Immigration and Nationality Act, as well as satisfactory completion of any background check, reference check, or other pre-employment screening required by the Company's standard onboarding procedures.
