# legalbench-rule-application pack — Client letter: Meridian Grain Cooperative evidentiary scenarios for trial preparation

MERIDIAN GRAIN COOPERATIVE
Office of the General Counsel — Dorrance, Kansas

To our trial team at Whitlock & Deene LLP:

As we prepare for trial in the north silo fire case (Meridian Grain Cooperative v. Aldermoor Fan & Blower Co.), please screen the following three evidentiary items. For each, we state exactly what the item is and the precise purpose for which we intend to offer it. Please analyze each scenario separately under the firm's evidence memo.

SCENARIO A. To prove that the north silo exhaust fan was in fact defective, we intend to have warehouse worker Tobias Prell testify that, on the day after the fire, foreman Della Kite told him: 'the north silo fan has been shorting for weeks.' Ms. Kite made this remark in the loading yard, not in any court proceeding. Our purpose in offering the remark is to prove that the fan really had been shorting — that is, to prove the truth of what Ms. Kite said.

SCENARIO B. Separately, to prove that Aldermoor's regional operations manager, Petra Vosniak, was on notice of a possible defect two weeks before the fire, we intend to show that Ms. Kite made the very same remark — 'the north silo fan has been shorting for weeks' — directly to Ms. Vosniak at the February vendor meeting. For this item, we do not care whether the remark was accurate. Our sole stated purpose is to show the effect on the listener: that having heard the warning, Ms. Vosniak was on notice and had a duty to inspect. We are NOT offering this item to prove the fan was actually shorting.

SCENARIO C. Fire investigator Ruth Ohanian interviewed night watchman Arno Besh at the scene. Mr. Besh could not speak due to smoke inhalation. Investigator Ohanian asked him directly: 'Did you see the fan spark before the flames started?' Mr. Besh looked at her and deliberately nodded his head in response to the question. We intend to offer testimony about the nod to prove that the fan did spark before the flames started — that is, to prove the truth of what Mr. Besh communicated by nodding. The nod occurred at the fire scene, out of court.

Please record one determination per scenario as your engagement instructions direct, and note that our statements above about the purpose of each offer are final; the trial committee has locked them.

Very truly yours,
Halima Droste, General Counsel
Meridian Grain Cooperative
