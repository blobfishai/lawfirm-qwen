# MAUD deal points pack — Silverline Data Systems deal protection and termination note to file (Sections 5.1, 5.3, 7.3 and 8.9)

NOTE TO FILE — Silverline Data Systems / Aurelian Summit Group: deal protection, interim covenant and termination provisions, conformed from the execution version of the Agreement and Plan of Merger dated July 20, 2026 (Sections 5.1, 5.3, 7.3 and 8.9). Prepared by Ellsworth & Crane LLP for the deal-points survey grid. For the avoidance of doubt in the survey grid: this agreement contains NO go-shop provision of any kind; the non-solicitation covenant applies from execution of the Agreement, and there is no post-signing window during which the Company may solicit competing proposals.

Section 5.1 Interim Operating Covenant. From the date of the Agreement until the earlier of the Effective Time and termination, except with Parent's prior written consent (not to be unreasonably withheld), the Company shall use commercially reasonable efforts to conduct its business in the ordinary course of business and to preserve intact its material business relationships. Drafting note: unlike the market-majority formulation, this covenant does NOT include a "consistent with past practice" qualifier.

Section 5.3 No Solicitation; Fiduciary Exception; Matching Rights. From and after the date of the Agreement, the Company shall not, and shall cause its Representatives not to, solicit, initiate or knowingly facilitate any Acquisition Proposal. Prior to the Acceptance Time, in response to a bona fide unsolicited written Acquisition Proposal that the Company Board determines in good faith (after consultation with outside counsel and its financial advisor) constitutes or would reasonably be expected to lead to a Superior Proposal, and where failure to act would be inconsistent with the directors' fiduciary duties under applicable Law, the Company may furnish information under an Acceptable Confidentiality Agreement and engage in discussions. Before any Adverse Recommendation Change or fiduciary termination, the Company must give Parent five (5) Business Days' prior written notice and negotiate in good faith during that period (the "Match Period"); each subsequent material modification to the financial terms of the Superior Proposal triggers a new notice and an additional two (2) Business Days' Match Period.

Section 7.3 Company Termination Fee. Upon (a) a fiduciary termination by the Company to accept a Superior Proposal, (b) termination by Parent following an Adverse Recommendation Change, or (c) a tail-period transaction (an Acquisition Proposal publicly made before termination and consummated, or made the subject of a definitive agreement, within nine (9) months after termination for Outside Date failure or Company breach), the Company shall pay Parent a termination fee of $86,500,000 in cash (the "Company Termination Fee"), payable at most once.

Section 8.9 Remedies. The parties' remedies are asymmetric. Parent and Purchaser are entitled to specific performance of the Company's covenants under Sections 5.1 and 5.3. The Company, however, is NOT entitled to specific performance to compel Parent or Purchaser to commence or consummate the Offer or to effect the Closing; the Company's sole and exclusive remedy against Parent and Purchaser for any failure to consummate the Offer or the Merger is an action for monetary damages, capped at $150,000,000 in the aggregate (the "Parent Liability Cap"). No separate reverse termination fee is provided.
