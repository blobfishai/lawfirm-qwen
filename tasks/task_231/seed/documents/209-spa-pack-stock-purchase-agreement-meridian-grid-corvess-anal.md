STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (DRAFT) by and among Meridian Grid Holdings, Inc. ("Buyer"), Talvane Energy Partners, LLC ("Seller"), and Corvess Analytics, Inc. (the "Company").

ARTICLE II — PURCHASE AND SALE (DRAFT TERMS)

Section 2.1 Purchase Price. The aggregate consideration for the Shares shall be Eighty Million Dollars ($80,000,000) (the "Base Purchase Price") [Seller note: Seller continues to seek $86,000,000; bracketed pending valuation call].

Section 2.2 Escrow. Buyer shall deposit Six Million Dollars ($6,000,000) as the Indemnity Escrow Amount [Buyer proposed 7.5% of the Base Purchase Price; Seller countered 6.0%] and One Million Dollars ($1,000,000) as the Adjustment Escrow Amount, in each case with an escrow agent to be agreed.

Section 2.3 [RESERVED — Buyer's first draft contained an earn-out of up to $5,000,000 tied to 2026 booked revenue; Seller rejected any contingent consideration and this concept was dropped from subsequent drafts.]

ARTICLE VIII — INDEMNIFICATION (DRAFT TERMS)

Section 8.1 Survival. Non-fundamental representations and warranties to survive for twenty-four (24) months following the Closing Date [Seller markup: reduce to 15 months; compromise to be discussed].

Section 8.2 Cap. Seller's aggregate indemnification liability not to exceed Twelve Million Dollars ($12,000,000) [Seller markup: 12% of Base Purchase Price].

Section 8.3 Basket. Deductible of Four Hundred Thousand Dollars ($400,000) [structure — deductible vs. tipping — still open in this draft].

ARTICLE X — MISCELLANEOUS (DRAFT)

Section 10.6 Governing Law. [Open issue in this draft: Buyer proposes Delaware; Seller's Texas-based tax counsel raised Texas law for the escrow agreement only. Resolved in favor of Delaware in the execution version.]
