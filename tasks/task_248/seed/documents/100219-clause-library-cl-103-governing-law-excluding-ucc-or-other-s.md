# Clause library — CL-103 Governing Law excluding UCC or other similar regulatory frameworks

PRECEDENT CLAUSE CL-103
Category: Governing Law excluding UCC or other similar regulatory frameworks
Library slug: governing_law_carveout
Approved by: Contracts KM Committee

GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, expressly excluding the Uniform Commercial Code and any other body of law that would apply the law of another jurisdiction to the parties' dealings.

Use note: this is firm-approved precedent language; deviations require playbook escalation.
