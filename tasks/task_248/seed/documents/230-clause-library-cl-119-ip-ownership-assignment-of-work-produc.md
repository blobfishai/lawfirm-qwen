# Clause library — CL-119 IP Ownership / assignment of work product

PRECEDENT CLAUSE CL-119
Category: IP Ownership / assignment of work product
Library slug: ip_ownership_assignment
Approved by: Contracts KM Committee

IP OWNERSHIP. All Deliverables and work product created by Supplier specifically for Customer under a Statement of Work shall be deemed works made for hire and, to the extent not so deemed, are hereby irrevocably assigned to Customer, excluding Supplier's pre-existing materials, which are licensed to Customer on a perpetual, non-exclusive basis.

Use note: this is firm-approved precedent language; deviations require playbook escalation.
