# Clause library — CL-114 Renewal clause that requires notice to renew

PRECEDENT CLAUSE CL-114
Category: Renewal clause that requires notice to renew
Library slug: renewal_notice_required
Approved by: Contracts KM Committee

RENEWAL. This Agreement shall renew for successive twelve (12) month terms only upon Customer's written notice of renewal delivered not less than thirty (30) days prior to the end of the then-current Term; absent such notice the Agreement shall expire.

Use note: this is firm-approved precedent language; deviations require playbook escalation.
