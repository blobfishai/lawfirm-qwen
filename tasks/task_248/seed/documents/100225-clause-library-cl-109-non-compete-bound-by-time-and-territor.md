# Clause library — CL-109 Non-compete bound by time and territory

PRECEDENT CLAUSE CL-109
Category: Non-compete bound by time and territory
Library slug: non_compete_time_territory
Approved by: Contracts KM Committee

RESTRICTIVE COVENANT. For a period of twenty-four (24) months following the Closing Date, Seller shall not, within the states of New York, New Jersey, and Connecticut, engage in any business that competes with the Business as conducted on the Closing Date.

Use note: this is firm-approved precedent language; deviations require playbook escalation.
