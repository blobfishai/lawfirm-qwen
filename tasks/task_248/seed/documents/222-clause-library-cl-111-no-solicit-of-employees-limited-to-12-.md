# Clause library — CL-111 No-Solicit of Employees limited to 12 months

PRECEDENT CLAUSE CL-111
Category: No-Solicit of Employees limited to 12 months
Library slug: no_solicit_employees_12
Approved by: Contracts KM Committee

NON-SOLICITATION OF EMPLOYEES. For twelve (12) months following termination, neither party shall directly solicit for employment any employee of the other party with whom it had material contact, provided that general advertisements not targeted at such employees shall not constitute solicitation.

Use note: this is firm-approved precedent language; deviations require playbook escalation.
