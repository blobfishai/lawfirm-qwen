# Regulatory pack — Conduct of Business Chapter 3: Client Classification (excerpt)

CHAPTER 3 — CLIENT CLASSIFICATION

3.4.1 An Authorised Person must classify each client as a Retail Client, a Professional Client, or a Market Counterparty before providing a regulated service.

3.4.7 A client may be treated as a Professional Client only where the Authorised Person has undertaken an assessment of the client's knowledge, experience, and expertise and is reasonably satisfied that the client is capable of making its own investment decisions and understanding the risks involved.

3.4.9 An Authorised Person must retain the classification assessment record for a period of SIX (6) YEARS from the date the client relationship ends.
