# legalbench-rule-application pack — Client letter: Ostrander Tooling multi-forum suit intake (jurisdiction scenarios)

OSTRANDER TOOLING WORKS, INC.
Canton, Ohio — Office of the President

To the Civil Procedure Group, Whitlock & Deene LLP:

Ostrander Tooling Works, Inc. is an Ohio corporation with its only plant and headquarters in Canton, Ohio; treat Ostrander as a citizen of Ohio and domiciled in Ohio for all scenarios. We have four forum questions. Please analyze each numbered scenario independently under the firm's jurisdiction memo and record one determination per scenario as your assignment directs.

SCENARIO 1 (personal jurisdiction — proposed suit in Ohio). We wish to sue Karvella Presses LLC, an Indiana company with its principal place of business in Fort Wayne, Indiana, in an Ohio court for breach of our hydraulic press supply contract. The relevant history: Karvella's sales director traveled to Ohio six separate times over eight months to demonstrate press models on our Canton floor and to negotiate terms, and Karvella signed the supply contract at our Canton offices. The breach we would sue over is Karvella's failure to deliver the very presses ordered under that contract.

SCENARIO 2 (personal jurisdiction — proposed suit in Ohio). We also wish to sue Brumfeld Freight Lines, a Georgia carrier with its principal place of business in Savannah, Georgia, in an Ohio court over a loading-dock injury to our purchasing agent that occurred entirely at Brumfeld's Savannah terminal during a site visit. Brumfeld has no operations in Ohio, no Ohio customers, no Ohio employees, and solicits no business in Ohio. Its only connection to Ohio that our investigator could find: one Brumfeld driver once drove through Ohio in transit on a Pennsylvania-to-Kentucky route unrelated to our claim.

SCENARIO 3 (diversity jurisdiction — proposed federal suit). Ostrander (Ohio citizen, sole plaintiff) intends to sue Quenneville Alloys Inc., which is incorporated in Delaware and has its principal place of business in Louisville, Kentucky, as sole defendant, for supplying out-of-specification alloy stock. Our damages demand is $410,000, exclusive of interest and costs.

SCENARIO 4 (diversity jurisdiction — proposed federal suit). Ostrander (Ohio citizen, sole plaintiff) intends to sue Talwick Machining Corp., which is incorporated in Michigan and has its principal place of business in Lansing, Michigan, as sole defendant, over a rejected tooling order. Our accountants have fixed the damages demand at exactly $75,000.00, exclusive of interest and costs, and the board will not authorize pleading a higher figure.

With thanks,
Edwin Ostrander III, President
