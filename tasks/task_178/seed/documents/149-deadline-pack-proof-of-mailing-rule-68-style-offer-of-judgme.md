# Deadline pack — Proof of Mailing: Rule 68-style Offer of Judgment served by mail September 18, 2026

PROOF OF SERVICE BY MAIL (SIMULATED)

Case No. 26-CV-01847. On Friday, September 18, 2026, Talvern Logistics Group served on Meridian Grain Cooperative, BY FIRST-CLASS MAIL ONLY, an Offer of Judgment in the amount of $1,450,000 inclusive of costs, deposited with postage prepaid at the Harborview station.

Under the Consolidated Rules Memo the offer may be accepted within 14 days after service; because service was made BY MAIL, the 3-day mail adjustment of SRCP 6(d) applies AFTER the 14-day period is computed, and the weekend/holiday rollover applies after the mail days are added.

No electronic service of the offer was made. The register entry for the acceptance deadline is the controlling calendar date for the client's decision meeting.
