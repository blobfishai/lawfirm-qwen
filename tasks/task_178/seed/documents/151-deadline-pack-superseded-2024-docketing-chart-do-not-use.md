# Deadline pack — SUPERSEDED 2024 Docketing Chart (do not use)

2024 DOCKETING QUICK CHART — WITHDRAWN, RETAINED FOR ARCHIVE ONLY

This chart was withdrawn firm-wide after the docketing audit. It used the OLD counting method: count the trigger day as day one, apply a 5-day mail adjustment before rollover, and treat only January 1, July 4, and December 25 as court holidays. Example rows (WRONG under current SRCP 6): answer to complaint served June 3 → 'June 23'; opposition to motion filed August 14 → 'August 27'; offer of judgment served by mail September 18 → 'October 7'; notice of appeal from November 20 judgment → 'December 19'.

Every date in this chart is superseded by the Consolidated Rules Memo. Associates found relying on this chart are subject to remedial docketing training.
