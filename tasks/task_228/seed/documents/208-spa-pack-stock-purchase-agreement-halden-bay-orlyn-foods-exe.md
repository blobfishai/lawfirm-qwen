# SPA pack — Stock Purchase Agreement (Halden Bay / Orlyn Foods) execution version

STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT — EXECUTION VERSION

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "Agreement") is made as of April 3, 2026, by and among Halden Bay Capital Acquisition Corp., a Delaware corporation ("Buyer"), Renwick Orlyn Holdings LLC, a New York limited liability company ("Principal Seller"), the other holders listed on Schedule A (together with Principal Seller, the "Sellers"), and Orlyn Foods Group, Inc., a New York corporation (the "Company").

RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Sellers collectively own all 1,150,000 issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of the Company (the "Shares"); and WHEREAS, the Sellers desire to sell, and Buyer desires to purchase, all of the Shares.

ARTICLE II — PURCHASE AND SALE

Section 2.1 Base Purchase Price. The aggregate base consideration for the Shares shall be Fifty-Two Million Five Hundred Thousand Dollars ($52,500,000) (the "Base Purchase Price"), payable at Closing as set forth in the Funds Flow Memorandum.

Section 2.2 Escrow. At the Closing, Buyer shall withhold from the Base Purchase Price and deposit Four Million Dollars ($4,000,000) (the "Indemnity Escrow Amount") with Lakemont Escrow Services LLC, as escrow agent, to be held in escrow for twelve (12) months following the Closing Date as the sole and exclusive source of recovery for indemnification claims under Section 9.1(a), other than claims based on Fraud.

Section 2.3 Earn-Out. In addition to the Base Purchase Price, Buyer shall pay to the Sellers contingent consideration of up to Ten Million Dollars ($10,000,000) (the "Earn-Out"), payable in accordance with Exhibit D if the Company's Net Revenue for the fiscal year ending December 31, 2027 equals or exceeds Sixty-One Million Dollars ($61,000,000). Fifty percent (50%) of the Earn-Out is payable at a Net Revenue threshold of $56,000,000, with straight-line interpolation between the thresholds.

ARTICLE VII — CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

Buyer's obligation to close is conditioned upon: (a) the accuracy of the Sellers' representations and warranties, subject to the Material Adverse Effect standard set forth in Section 7.2; (b) receipt of the consent of Brantley Cold Chain Logistics, Inc. under the Master Distribution Agreement; (c) payoff letters with respect to the Vantera Credit Facility; and (d) delivery of the executed Escrow Agreement, FIRPTA certificates, and the Restrictive Covenant Agreements of each Key Employee.

ARTICLE IX — INDEMNIFICATION

Section 9.1 Survival. The representations and warranties in Article IV and Article V (other than the Fundamental Representations) shall survive the Closing for twelve (12) months following the Closing Date, at which time they shall expire together with any right to assert a claim not previously noticed. The Fundamental Representations shall survive for six (6) years following the Closing Date.

Section 9.2 Cap. Except with respect to Fundamental Representations and Fraud, the aggregate liability of the Sellers under this Article IX shall not exceed Seven Million Eight Hundred Seventy-Five Thousand Dollars ($7,875,000) (the "Indemnity Cap").

Section 9.3 Basket. The Sellers shall not be liable under Section 9.1(a) unless the aggregate amount of Losses exceeds Two Hundred Sixty-Two Thousand Five Hundred Dollars ($262,500) (the "Threshold"), in which case the Sellers shall be liable for all such Losses from the first dollar, and not merely the excess. The Threshold is a tipping basket.

ARTICLE XI — MISCELLANEOUS

Section 11.4 Governing Law; Venue. This Agreement and all disputes arising hereunder shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without giving effect to any choice-of-law rules that would apply the law of another jurisdiction. The parties consent to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts sitting in New York County, New York, and waive any objection to venue therein.

Section 11.7 Transfer Taxes. All transfer, documentary, and stamp taxes shall be borne fifty percent (50%) by Buyer and fifty percent (50%) by the Sellers.
