GRIDCORE SOFTWARE LICENSE AGREEMENT (EXECUTION COPY)

This Software License Agreement (this "Agreement"), dated as of June 2, 2025, is entered into by and between Helix Grid, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Licensor"), and Tessellate Systems Ltd, a private limited company organized under the laws of England and Wales ("Licensee"). This execution copy supersedes in its entirety the superseded draft v2 dated April 18, 2025, and all other prior drafts.

1. Definitions. "Software" means Licensor's GridCore optimization engine, version 5.x, in object code form, together with its documentation. "Field" means maritime logistics scheduling and port-call optimization. "Net Revenue" means Licensee's gross revenue from Licensee products embedding the Software, less returns and taxes.

2. License Grant; Exclusivity. Licensor hereby grants to Licensee a perpetual and irrevocable license to use, reproduce, and distribute the Software solely within the Field. The license granted under this Section 2 is personal to Licensee and is non-transferable and non-sublicensable: Licensee may not assign, transfer, or sublicense the license, in whole or in part, to any third party without Licensor's prior written consent. During the term, Licensor shall not license, sell, or otherwise make the Software available to any person other than Licensee for use within the Field; this exclusive dealing commitment is a material inducement to Licensee's execution of this Agreement.

3. Royalties; Revenue Sharing. In consideration of the license, Licensee shall pay Licensor a running royalty equal to four percent (4%) of Net Revenue, payable within forty-five (45) days after the end of each calendar quarter. There is no minimum royalty and no minimum purchase, order, or usage commitment of any kind under this Agreement, and no volume-based fee increase or usage threshold applies.

4. Most Favored Licensee. If, during the term, Licensor grants any third party a license to the Software on royalty rates or fee terms more favorable than those set forth in Section 3, Licensor shall notify Licensee within thirty (30) days, and such more favorable terms shall automatically be extended to Licensee, retroactive to the effective date of the third-party grant.

5. Source Code Escrow. Within thirty (30) days after the Effective Date, Licensor shall deposit the source code of the Software with Iron Vault Fiduciary Services LLC pursuant to a three-party escrow agreement. The deposit shall be updated with each major release and shall be released to Licensee upon Licensor's bankruptcy, insolvency, general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or cessation of business operations.

6. Limited Warranty. Licensor warrants that, for a period of twelve (12) months following initial delivery, the Software will conform in all material respects to its documentation. Licensee's exclusive remedy for breach of this warranty is repair, replacement, or, if neither is commercially practicable, refund of royalties paid for the affected period.

7. Covenant Not to Sue; No Challenge. Licensee covenants that it shall not, during the term and for three (3) years thereafter, bring or voluntarily assist any action contesting the validity, enforceability, or Licensor's ownership of any patent, copyright, or trade secret embodied in the Software.

8. Term; Termination. This Agreement continues in perpetuity unless terminated by a party for a material breach that remains uncured sixty (60) days after written notice. Neither party has any right to terminate this Agreement for convenience or without cause. The license granted in Section 2, being irrevocable, survives any termination other than termination by Licensor for Licensee's uncured payment breach.

9. Insurance. For the avoidance of doubt, neither party is required by this Agreement to obtain or maintain any insurance for the benefit of the other party.
