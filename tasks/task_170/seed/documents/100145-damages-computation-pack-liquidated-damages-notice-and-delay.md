# damages-computation pack — Liquidated Damages Notice and Delay Certification (Corvess Plant Expansion Contract)

LIQUIDATED DAMAGES NOTICE AND DELAY CERTIFICATION

Re: Corvess Plant Expansion Contract — Design-Build Agreement between Corvess Industrial Group, LLC ("Owner") and Talley Constructors, Inc. ("Contractor")

This Notice is issued by Owner pursuant to Section 11.4 of the Design-Build Agreement (the "Contract") and certifies the delay period for purposes of the liquidated damages assessment now tracked as a deadline-exposure item in the litigation file.

Section 1. Contractual Liquidated Damages Terms (quoted from Contract § 11.4).
1.1 Rate. "For each calendar day that Substantial Completion occurs after the Guaranteed Completion Date, Contractor shall pay Owner liquidated damages of $4,500 per calendar day."
1.2 Cap. "Contractor's aggregate liability for liquidated damages under this Section 11.4 shall not exceed $315,000, being seven percent (7%) of the original Contract Price of $4,500,000."
1.3 Exclusivity. Liquidated damages under § 11.4 are Owner's sole remedy for delay to Substantial Completion, and are subject to the cap in 1.2 in all circumstances.

Section 2. Delay Certification.
2.1 The Guaranteed Completion Date under the Contract was March 1.
2.2 Substantial Completion, as certified by the independent engineer, was achieved on May 15.
2.3 The parties' project representatives have jointly certified, and this Notice adopts, a delay period of exactly 75 (seventy-five) calendar days for purposes of the § 11.4 assessment. No excusable-delay credits remain outstanding; all time-extension requests have been resolved and are reflected in the certified 75-day figure.

Section 3. Assessment Method.
3.1 The assessed liquidated damages equal the certified delay period in 2.3 multiplied by the daily rate in 1.1, PROVIDED THAT if the product exceeds the cap stated in 1.2, the assessment is limited to the cap. The capped result, rounded to whole dollars, is the deadline exposure to be recorded.
3.2 Owner reserves all rights with respect to warranty claims and non-delay defaults, which are outside § 11.4 and outside the cap.

Section 4. Payment Demand.
4.1 Owner demands payment of the assessed amount within 30 days of this Notice. Failure to pay will result in a draw on the performance security in the amount of the assessment.

Issued by Owner's authorized representative, with a copy to the independent engineer and to counsel of record.
