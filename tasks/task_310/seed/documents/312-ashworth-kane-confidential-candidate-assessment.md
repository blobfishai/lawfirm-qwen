I. Engagement Overview and Search Summary
Ashworth Kane Partners, LLC ("Ashworth Kane" or the "Firm") was retained by Verdana Technologies, Inc. ("Verdana" or the "Company") in October 2024 to conduct a confidential executive search for the inaugural Senior Vice President, Product Engineering — Autonomous Analytics Division (the "Role"). The engagement was authorized by Priya Ramanathan, Chief People Officer, with the concurrence of Verdana's Chief Executive Officer and, subsequently, the Board of Directors' Compensation Committee. This assessment is furnished in connection with that engagement and presents Ashworth Kane's recommendation regarding the candidacy of Dr. Mariana Chen-Velasco.
Engagement Economics. The engagement was structured as a fixed retainer of $175,000, fully earned upon execution of the search engagement agreement, plus a placement fee equal to 25% of the placed candidate's Year 1 total cash compensation (defined as base salary plus target annual bonus). The retainer has been earned in full. The placement fee will be due upon the candidate's acceptance of a fully authorized employment offer from Verdana.
Search Objective. The primary objective of the engagement is to identify and deliver a senior technical executive capable of building and scaling a net-new product division within Verdana's enterprise SaaS platform. The Role reports directly to the Chief Executive Officer and carries accountability for the Board-approved first-year revenue target of $14,000,000 and the three-year revenue plan of $62,000,000 for the Autonomous Analytics division. The successful candidate must combine deep technical expertise in autonomous systems, artificial intelligence, and machine learning with a demonstrated track record of standing up revenue-generating product organizations at enterprise scale. The Board further specified that the ideal candidate should possess the executive presence, market credibility, and investor-readiness profile appropriate for a company that may pursue an initial public offering or strategic transaction within the next 18 to 24 months, as indicated by Ridgeline Partners, Inc., Verdana's lead financial advisor.
Scope of Search. The search was national in scope with targeted international outreach to select markets, including Toronto, London, and Bangalore, where concentrations of relevant talent were identified during the research phase. The Firm's proprietary research function surveyed the enterprise AI/ML, autonomous systems, and advanced analytics product leadership landscape, with particular emphasis on candidates drawn from enterprise SaaS companies in the $1 billion to $3 billion valuation range and adjacent technology sectors, including infrastructure-layer AI/ML platform companies and vertical analytics providers serving regulated industries.
Duration. The search has been active for approximately seven months, from October 2024 through the date of this assessment in May 2025. This duration reflects the highly specialized nature of the role, the limited pool of qualified candidates who combine the requisite technical depth with division-building experience, and the competitive market dynamics that have characterized senior AI/ML leadership hiring throughout the 2024–2025 cycle.
Candidate Pipeline Summary. The research phase yielded an initial universe of approximately 180 prospective candidates across the target sectors and geographies. Through Ashworth Kane's multi-stage qualification process — encompassing proprietary database research, direct sourcing outreach, referral network activation, and preliminary competency screening — the field was narrowed to 23 candidates who met the threshold qualifications for the Role. Of those 23 qualified prospects, 14 entered preliminary discussions with the Firm's engagement team, during which their interest, availability, compensation expectations, and cultural alignment were assessed. Five candidates advanced to finalist status and were presented to Verdana's hiring team for in-depth evaluation.
The following sections of this assessment describe the internal successor analysis, the comparative ranking of the five finalists, the detailed profile and assessment of the top-ranked candidate, the strategic urgency and cost-of-loss analysis, the negotiation dynamics, and Ashworth Kane's formal recommendation.
II. Internal Successor Analysis
In coordination with Priya Ramanathan, Chief People Officer, and the broader Verdana executive leadership team, Ashworth Kane conducted a parallel internal talent review concurrent with the external search. This review was designed to identify any internal candidates who might be ready-now successors for the SVP, Product Engineering — Autonomous Analytics Role or who might be positioned to assume the Role within a timeline consistent with the Board-approved launch plan.
Finding. Zero internal ready-now successors were identified for the SVP, Product Engineering — Autonomous Analytics Role within Verdana's current employee population of approximately 2,400 individuals.
Contributing Factors. The absence of internal ready-now successors is attributable to the distinctive combination of competencies required by the Role. Specifically, the position demands deep domain expertise in autonomous and AI/ML-driven product engineering at enterprise scale, combined with prior experience standing up a revenue-generating division from inception — including full lifecycle responsibility for product strategy, engineering team build-out, go-to-market alignment, and P&L ownership. This composite profile is not currently represented in Verdana's leadership bench. While Verdana employs a number of technically accomplished engineering leaders, none possesses the intersection of autonomous analytics domain depth, division-building experience, and executive-level P&L accountability that the Role requires. Furthermore, the Role carries direct CEO reporting and Board-level interaction responsibilities that presuppose a level of executive presence and investor-readiness not yet developed in the current internal pipeline.
Development Pipeline Assessment. Ashworth Kane's internal review, conducted jointly with Verdana's People Operations team and informed by the Company's most recent calibration and succession data, identified a small number of internal development pipeline candidates — individuals who, with targeted development and broadening assignments, could potentially grow into a role of this scope. However, these candidates were assessed as 18 to 24 months from readiness at minimum, a timeline that is inconsistent with the Board-approved launch plan for the Autonomous Analytics division and would result in unacceptable delays to the division's first-year revenue target. The Board's expectation is that the SVP, Product Engineering will be in seat and operational within 60 to 90 days of offer acceptance, a timeline that necessitates an external hire with relevant prior experience.
In light of these findings, Ashworth Kane proceeded with the external search as the sole viable path to filling the Role within the required timeline.
III. Finalist Overview and Comparative Ranking
Following the completion of the research, outreach, screening, and preliminary assessment phases described in Section I, Ashworth Kane advanced five candidates to finalist status. Each finalist was evaluated against Ashworth Kane's proprietary assessment framework, which scores candidates across five critical dimensions: (1) Technical and Domain Expertise, (2) Leadership and Division-Building Capability, (3) Strategic and Commercial Acumen, (4) Cultural and Organizational Fit, and (5) Availability and Execution Readiness. Domain Fit Scores referenced below are derived from the Firm's proprietary methodology, which weights technical competency alignment, market-segment relevance, and prior comparable-role experience on a 100-point scale.
The five finalists are presented below in ranked order. Dr. Chen-Velasco, the top-ranked candidate, is identified by name; the remaining four candidates are anonymized in accordance with Ashworth Kane's standard practice for confidential candidate assessments.
Candidate 1 (Top-Ranked): Dr. Mariana Chen-Velasco
Dr. Chen-Velasco is rated "Exceptional" across all five assessment dimensions. She is the unambiguous top-ranked candidate in this search and the only finalist who satisfies all critical requirements of the Role simultaneously. A comprehensive profile and assessment of Dr. Chen-Velasco is provided in Section IV below.
Candidate 2
Candidate 2 is rated "Strong" overall and presents a credible, though materially less compelling, alternative to Dr. Chen-Velasco. This candidate possesses solid engineering leadership credentials and relevant experience in enterprise AI/ML product development. However, Candidate 2 is materially weaker in several critical areas: (a) patent portfolio — Candidate 2 holds two issued patents compared to Dr. Chen-Velasco's eleven, and those patents are in adjacent but not directly overlapping technical domains; (b) division-building experience — Candidate 2 has led significant engineering teams but has not previously stood up a net-new product division from inception within an established enterprise platform, which is the precise mandate of the Autonomous Analytics Role; and (c) go-to-market readiness — Candidate 2's experience has been concentrated on the engineering side of the product lifecycle, with limited direct accountability for revenue outcomes or cross-functional alignment with sales and marketing organizations at enterprise scale.
In addition, Candidate 2's availability presents a significant timeline risk. The candidate is currently subject to an employment agreement with a 90-day notice period and a 12-month non-compete clause. Ashworth Kane's preliminary assessment, informed by discussions with the candidate and general market intelligence, estimates that resolution of the non-compete obligation — whether through negotiation, waiver, or expiration — would require 5 to 7 months from the date of offer acceptance. This delay would have material implications for the Autonomous Analytics division's launch timeline and first-year revenue target, as detailed in Section V below.
Candidate 3
Candidate 3 brings substantial technical depth in machine learning engineering and has operated at significant scale within a large post-IPO enterprise. However, the candidate's leadership experience has been predominantly within established, mature product organizations rather than in building new divisions. Additionally, the candidate's domain expertise, while strong in ML engineering, lacks the specific depth in autonomous analytics and real-time inference architectures that the Autonomous Analytics division's technical roadmap demands. Ashworth Kane does not recommend advancing Candidate 3 to a final-round interview without significant caveats regarding the gap between the candidate's prior operating context and the entrepreneurial, division-building demands of the Role.
Candidate 4
Candidate 4 demonstrates strong product leadership skills and relevant exposure to autonomous systems. However, the candidate's experience is predominantly in a smaller-scale environment, and the transition to Verdana's enterprise platform — with its approximately 2,400-person organization, complex cross-functional dependencies, and Board-level reporting requirements — represents a significant step-up in leadership scope. The candidate's engineering depth, while adequate, does not approach the level required to serve as the technical authority for the Autonomous Analytics division's architecture decisions. Ashworth Kane rates this candidate as "Qualified" but does not recommend advancement to the final round.
Candidate 5
Candidate 5 brings the deepest bench of overall years of experience among the finalists and has broad exposure to analytics use cases across multiple industries. However, the candidate's career has been spent primarily in consulting and advisory contexts rather than in product-driven enterprise SaaS environments. The transition to an operating role with P&L accountability, engineering team management at scale, and direct responsibility for a Board-approved revenue target presents considerable risk. While the candidate's availability is favorable, Ashworth Kane does not recommend this candidate for the Role and included this individual in the finalist slate solely for completeness and comparability.
Conclusion. Dr. Mariana Chen-Velasco is the only finalist who satisfies all critical requirements of the Role simultaneously. No other candidate in the finalist pool — nor, in Ashworth Kane's assessment, any candidate identified during the broader 180-prospect research phase — presents an equivalent combination of technical domain expertise, division-building experience, leadership scale, competitive-market fluency, and availability. The gap between Dr. Chen-Velasco and the next-best candidate is substantial and, in Ashworth Kane's professional judgment, not bridgeable through onboarding, supplemental hiring, or other compensatory measures within the timeline required by Verdana's Board-approved launch plan.
IV. Candidate Profile — Dr. Mariana Chen-Velasco
A. Professional Background and Qualifications
Dr. Mariana Chen-Velasco brings 18 years of progressive enterprise AI/ML product development and engineering leadership experience to the SVP, Product Engineering — Autonomous Analytics candidacy. Her career trajectory reflects a consistent pattern of increasing scope, accountability, and technical depth, with particular concentration in the market segments most directly relevant to Verdana's Autonomous Analytics division.
Dr. Chen-Velasco is the inventor or co-inventor on 11 issued United States patents spanning autonomous data processing, real-time inference engines, and ML-driven analytics architectures. This patent portfolio is unusually deep for an executive-level candidate and positions Dr. Chen-Velasco as both a strategic leader and a hands-on technical authority — a combination that Ashworth Kane considers essential for the inaugural leader of a net-new product division that must simultaneously define its technical vision and build organizational credibility with engineering talent.
Current Role. Dr. Chen-Velasco currently serves as Senior Vice President, Product Engineering at Helios Data Systems, LLC ("Helios"), headquartered in San Jose, California. Helios is a direct competitor to Verdana Technologies in the enterprise analytics and data intelligence market, with an estimated valuation in the $1.5 billion to $2.2 billion range and approximately 1,800 employees. At Helios, Dr. Chen-Velasco built and scaled the core analytics product line from initial concept through general availability, a process that encompassed product strategy definition, engineering team recruitment and organization design, technical architecture decisions, and go-to-market alignment. Her current P&L responsibility encompasses both engineering and product management functions, with a combined organization of approximately 210 direct and indirect reports and an annual operating budget in excess of $45 million.
Prior Roles. Prior to Helios, Dr. Chen-Velasco held senior engineering leadership positions at two enterprise SaaS companies, one post-IPO and one pre-IPO through a successful strategic acquisition. At the post-IPO company, she led a 90-person engineering organization responsible for the company's real-time analytics platform, delivering three major product releases over a 30-month period and contributing to a doubling of the platform's annual recurring revenue. At the pre-IPO company, she served as Vice President of Engineering and was a member of the executive leadership team through the company's acquisition by a Fortune 500 technology conglomerate at an enterprise value of approximately $780 million. This breadth of experience — spanning concept-stage product development, scaling within an established platform, and navigating both IPO and M&A transaction environments — directly mirrors the full lifecycle demands of the Autonomous Analytics division mandate.
Education. Dr. Chen-Velasco holds a Ph.D. in Computer Science from Stanford University, with a doctoral research focus on distributed autonomous systems and real-time data inference. She also holds a B.S. in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology.
B. Technical and Domain Expertise
Dr. Chen-Velasco possesses deep domain authority in the three technical areas most critical to the Autonomous Analytics division's success: autonomous analytics, real-time ML inference, and enterprise-grade AI/ML pipeline architecture. Her technical expertise is not merely theoretical; it has been exercised repeatedly in the context of building, shipping, and scaling enterprise-grade products that serve demanding commercial customers.
Patent Portfolio. Dr. Chen-Velasco's portfolio of 11 issued patents is directly aligned with the technical roadmap for Verdana's Autonomous Analytics division. Several of these patents address core architectural challenges — including distributed inference orchestration, anomaly detection in high-velocity data streams, and adaptive model retraining in production environments — that Verdana's technical team has identified as foundational to the division's product architecture. The existence of this patent portfolio provides both credibility with prospective engineering hires (who will evaluate the technical vision of the division's leader as a key factor in their own career decisions) and potential intellectual property advantages that may be relevant to Verdana's competitive positioning and future IP strategy. Ashworth Kane notes that any assessment of the portability or applicability of these patents should be conducted by Verdana's General Counsel in coordination with appropriate patent counsel.
Thought Leadership. Dr. Chen-Velasco is recognized as a thought leader in the enterprise AI/ML community. She has delivered keynote addresses at three major industry conferences in the past 24 months, has published peer-reviewed research in leading journals, and currently serves on the technical advisory boards of two prominent enterprise AI/ML organizations. This visibility enhances her ability to attract engineering talent, build market credibility for the Autonomous Analytics division, and represent Verdana with authority in customer, partner, and investor contexts.
Technical Vision Alignment. Based on Ashworth Kane's in-depth technical discussions with Dr. Chen-Velasco and our parallel conversations with Verdana's Chief Technology Officer and VP of Engineering, we assess that Dr. Chen-Velasco's technical vision is specifically calibrated to the market segment Verdana's Autonomous Analytics division is designed to capture. Her perspective on the evolution of autonomous analytics — including the shift from batch-oriented to real-time inference, the integration of autonomous decision-making into enterprise workflows, and the importance of explainability and auditability in regulated-industry deployments — is closely aligned with the strategic direction Verdana's Board has approved for the division.
C. Leadership and Division-Building Capability
The SVP, Product Engineering — Autonomous Analytics Role is, at its core, a division-building mandate. The successful candidate must create a new product organization from the ground up within an established enterprise platform, assembling a team, defining a product strategy, establishing engineering processes, and delivering against a Board-approved revenue plan — all while navigating the organizational and cultural dynamics of a 2,400-person company with existing products, customers, and stakeholders.
Dr. Chen-Velasco has a proven track record of executing precisely this type of mandate. At Helios, she stood up the core analytics product line as a net-new initiative within an existing enterprise platform, growing the organization from a founding team of six engineers to a fully scaled product and engineering organization of over 200 professionals. This experience is directly transferable to the Autonomous Analytics division, where she would be expected to follow a similar trajectory — albeit with the advantage of Verdana's existing platform, customer base, and go-to-market infrastructure.
Talent Acquisition and Retention. Dr. Chen-Velasco has demonstrated a consistent ability to recruit and retain top-tier engineering talent in the highly competitive enterprise AI/ML hiring market. Her professional network spans the major technology hubs, including the San Francisco Bay Area, Seattle, New York, and Austin, and her reputation as a technical leader and culture-builder is expected to provide meaningful network hiring leverage in the early stages of the Autonomous Analytics division's team build-out. In Ashworth Kane's assessment, this network hiring capability is a significant and often underestimated source of value in division-building roles, as the quality and velocity of early hiring decisions disproportionately determine the trajectory of a new product organization.
Executive Operating Context. Dr. Chen-Velasco has operated as a senior executive within both pre-IPO and post-IPO environments, a background that is directly relevant given Ridgeline Partners, Inc.'s indication of a potential IPO or strategic transaction for Verdana within the next 18 to 24 months. She is experienced in Board-level reporting, investor presentations, and the governance and compliance requirements that intensify as a company approaches a public offering. She has also navigated the organizational complexity of integrating a new product division into an existing enterprise, managing stakeholder alignment across engineering, product, sales, marketing, and finance functions.
Strategic-to-Execution Translation. Dr. Chen-Velasco has repeatedly demonstrated the ability to translate Board-level strategic objectives into engineering execution plans with measurable revenue outcomes. At Helios, she was responsible for converting the Board's strategic commitment to the analytics product line into a detailed product roadmap, engineering staffing plan, and revenue model — and then executing against that plan to deliver results. This translation capability is the critical bridge between the Board's $14 million first-year revenue target and the day-to-day engineering and product decisions that will determine whether that target is achieved.
D. Current Employment and Competitive Context
A critical dimension of Dr. Chen-Velasco's candidacy is the competitive context in which it arises. Helios Data Systems, LLC is a direct competitor to Verdana Technologies in the enterprise data and analytics market. The two companies compete for many of the same enterprise customers, engineering talent, and market positioning narratives, and they operate in overlapping segments of the enterprise analytics value chain.
Competitive Implications. Dr. Chen-Velasco's departure from Helios would simultaneously strengthen Verdana's capabilities and remove a key technical leader from a competitive organization. This dual impact amplifies the strategic value of the hire beyond the direct contribution to the Autonomous Analytics division.
