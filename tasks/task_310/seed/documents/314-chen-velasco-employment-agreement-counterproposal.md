RECITALS
WHEREAS, Verdana Technologies, Inc. (the "Company") is a corporation duly organized and existing under the laws of the State of Delaware, with its principal offices located at 3200 Brazos Ridge Parkway, Suite 800, Austin, TX 78745;
WHEREAS, Dr. Mariana Chen-Velasco ("Executive") is an individual currently residing in the State of California;
WHEREAS, the Company desires to employ Executive in the position of Senior Vice President, Product Engineering – Autonomous Analytics, and Executive desires to accept such employment with the Company, on the terms and conditions set forth in this Executive Employment Agreement (this "Agreement");
WHEREAS, this Agreement supersedes all prior discussions, term sheets, offer letters, memoranda of understanding, and correspondence between the parties concerning Executive's employment with the Company, including without limitation all proposals and counter-proposals exchanged between the Company (and its counsel) and counsel for Executive (Fairgrove & Saldívar LLP) prior to the date hereof;
WHEREAS, Executive is currently employed by Helios Data Systems, LLC ("Helios"), and the parties acknowledge the need to address certain timing and transition obligations arising from Executive's current employment with Helios, including but not limited to Executive's notice obligations, the potential forfeiture of unvested compensation, and the applicability of any post-employment restrictive covenants to which Executive may be subject; and
WHEREAS, this counter-proposal draft was transmitted by Rebekah S. Otieno of Fairgrove & Saldívar LLP on May 28, 2025, on behalf of Executive, and reflects Executive's proposed modifications to the terms previously circulated by the Company.
NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements herein contained, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:
ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS
As used in this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Article 1 shall have the meanings ascribed to them elsewhere in this Agreement.
1.1 "Affiliate" shall mean any entity directly or indirectly controlling, controlled by, or under common control with the Company, where "control" means the possession, directly or indirectly, of the power to direct or cause the direction of the management and policies of such entity, whether through ownership of voting securities, by contract, or otherwise. For purposes of this definition, ownership of fifty percent (50%) or more of the outstanding voting securities of an entity shall be deemed to constitute control.
1.2 "Base Salary" shall have the meaning set forth in Section 4.1 of this Agreement and shall initially be Four Hundred Forty Thousand Dollars ($440,000) per annum.
1.3 "Board" shall mean the Board of Directors of Verdana Technologies, Inc., as constituted from time to time.
1.4 "Bonus Target" shall have the meaning set forth in Section 4.2(a) of this Agreement and shall initially be fifty-five percent (55%) of Base Salary, which, based on the initial Base Salary, equals Two Hundred Forty-Two Thousand Dollars ($242,000) per annum.
1.5 "Cause" shall have the meaning set forth in Section 7.2 of this Agreement.
1.6 "Change in Control" or "CIC" shall have the meaning set forth in Section 8.1 of this Agreement and shall encompass the following events: (a) the acquisition by any person or group (within the meaning of Section 13(d)(3) or 14(d)(2) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended) of more than fifty percent (50%) of the combined voting power of the then-outstanding voting securities of the Company entitled to vote generally in the election of directors; (b) the consummation of a merger, consolidation, statutory share exchange, or similar business combination transaction involving the Company, if, immediately after the consummation of such transaction, the stockholders of the Company immediately prior to such transaction hold less than fifty percent (50%) of the combined voting power of the outstanding voting securities of the surviving or resulting entity (or its ultimate parent entity); (c) the sale, transfer, or other disposition of all or substantially all of the assets of the Company; (d) a change in the composition of the Board such that the individuals who, as of the Effective Date, constitute the Board (together with any individual who becomes a director subsequent to the Effective Date whose election or nomination for election was approved by a vote of at least a majority of the then-incumbent directors) cease for any reason to constitute at least a majority of the Board; provided, however, that (e) the consummation of an initial public offering of the Company's securities ("IPO") shall not, standing alone, constitute a Change in Control.
1.7 "CIC Protection Period" shall have the meaning set forth in Section 8.2 of this Agreement and shall mean the period commencing three (3) months prior to, and ending twenty-four (24) months following, the consummation of a Change in Control.
1.8 "CIC Severance Amount" shall have the meaning set forth in Section 8.3(a) of this Agreement and shall equal two times (2.0×) the sum of Base Salary and Bonus Target, calculated as set forth therein.
1.9 "Compensation Committee" shall mean the Compensation Committee of the Board, or, if no such committee exists, the full Board or such other committee of the Board as may be designated to perform the functions of a compensation committee.
1.10 "Confidential Information" shall have the meaning set forth in Section 9.1 of this Agreement.
1.11 "Disability" shall have the meaning set forth in Section 7.4 of this Agreement and shall mean Executive's inability, by reason of a physical or mental impairment, to perform the essential functions of her position, with or without reasonable accommodation, for a period of one hundred eighty (180) consecutive calendar days or for a cumulative period of two hundred seventy (270) calendar days in any three hundred sixty-five (365)-day period, as determined by a licensed physician mutually agreed upon by the Company and Executive (or Executive's legal representative).
1.12 "Effective Date" shall mean the date on which Executive commences active employment with the Company, which date shall be mutually agreed upon by the parties but is targeted for no later than thirty (30) days following the full execution of this Agreement by both parties.
1.13 "Equity Award" shall mean the Initial RSU Grant described in Section 5.1 of this Agreement.
1.14 "Fiscal Year" shall mean the Company's fiscal year, which is currently the twelve (12)-month period commencing on January 1 and ending on December 31 of each calendar year, as may be changed by the Board from time to time.
1.15 "Good Reason" shall have the meaning set forth in Section 7.3 of this Agreement.
1.16 "Guaranteed Bonus Floor" shall have the meaning set forth in Section 4.2(b) of this Agreement and shall equal seventy-five percent (75%) of the Bonus Target for each of Executive's first two (2) Fiscal Years of employment, as further described therein.
1.17 "Release" shall have the meaning set forth in Section 7.6 of this Agreement and shall mean the Company's standard form of general release of claims, substantially in the form attached hereto as Exhibit A, to be provided to Executive within five (5) business days following the date of Executive's separation from service.
1.18 "Restricted Period" shall have the meaning set forth in Section 9.2 of this Agreement.
1.19 "RSU" shall mean a restricted stock unit granted under the Company's equity incentive plan, representing the right to receive one (1) share of the Company's common stock upon vesting, subject to the terms and conditions of such plan and the applicable award agreement.
1.20 "Section 409A" shall mean Section 409A of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended, and the Treasury Regulations and other interpretive guidance issued thereunder, including without limitation any such regulations or guidance that may be issued after the date of this Agreement.
1.21 "Target Bonus" shall be used synonymously with Bonus Target and shall equal Two Hundred Forty-Two Thousand Dollars ($242,000) per annum, based on the initial Base Salary of $440,000 multiplied by fifty-five percent (55%).
ARTICLE 2 — EMPLOYMENT AND DUTIES
Section 2.1 — Position and Title
The Company hereby agrees to employ Executive, and Executive hereby agrees to be employed by the Company, in the position of Senior Vice President, Product Engineering – Autonomous Analytics. Executive shall be a member of the Company's senior leadership team and shall report directly to the Chief Executive Officer of the Company (or such other C-suite officer as the Board may designate from time to time; provided, however, that any reassignment of Executive's reporting relationship to an officer below the C-suite level shall constitute a material diminution in reporting for purposes of the Good Reason definition set forth in Section 7.3). Executive's principal workplace shall be the Company's headquarters located at 3200 Brazos Ridge Parkway, Suite 800, Austin, TX 78745, subject to reasonable business travel requirements as may be necessary for the performance of Executive's duties.
Section 2.2 — Duties and Responsibilities
Executive shall have overall leadership responsibility for the Company's Autonomous Analytics product engineering division, including, without limitation, all related software engineering, systems architecture, machine learning operations (MLOps), data infrastructure, and product delivery functions within such division. Executive shall be responsible for setting the technical vision and strategic direction of the Autonomous Analytics division, building and managing the engineering team, collaborating with cross-functional stakeholders (including product management, design, sales, and customer success), and ensuring the timely and high-quality delivery of the division's product roadmap.
Executive shall devote substantially all of her professional time, attention, energy, and skill to the faithful and diligent performance of her duties hereunder, subject to Section 2.3 of this Agreement. Executive shall perform such duties as are customary for an officer of similar title and seniority at similarly situated technology companies of comparable size and stage, and such additional duties as may be reasonably assigned to her from time to time by the CEO, consistent with her position, title, and seniority.
Section 2.3 — Outside Activities
Notwithstanding the provisions of Section 2.2, Executive may, during the term of her employment, serve on up to two (2) outside boards of directors — one (1) for-profit board and one (1) nonprofit board — and may engage in reasonable civic, charitable, educational, and professional activities (including delivering lectures, teaching courses, and publishing scholarly articles), provided that such activities do not, individually or in the aggregate, materially interfere with the performance of Executive's duties hereunder or create an actual or apparent conflict of interest with the Company's business.
Prior written approval of the CEO shall be required before Executive accepts any for-profit board position. Such approval shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned, or delayed. Executive shall promptly notify the CEO if any outside activity that was previously approved becomes, or reasonably could be expected to become, a conflict of interest with the Company's business.
Section 2.4 — Representations of Executive
Executive represents and warrants to the Company that:
(a) She is not a party to any agreement, understanding, or obligation (including, without limitation, any non-competition agreement, non-solicitation agreement, confidentiality agreement, or similar restrictive covenant with Helios Data Systems, LLC or any other current or former employer) that would prevent or materially impair her ability to fulfill her obligations under this Agreement, except as Executive has previously disclosed to the Company in writing.
(b) She will not, in connection with her employment with the Company, bring to the Company, or use or disclose in the performance of her duties, any confidential or proprietary information, trade secrets, or intellectual property belonging to Helios Data Systems, LLC or any other prior employer, client, or third party, unless such information is publicly available through no fault of Executive or Executive has obtained express written authorization from the relevant party to use or disclose such information.
(c) She will comply with all applicable laws, regulations, and ethical standards in the performance of her duties hereunder.
ARTICLE 3 — TERM
Section 3.1 — At-Will Employment
Executive's employment with the Company shall be "at-will," meaning that either the Company or Executive may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without Cause, and with or without advance notice, subject to the severance, termination, and change in control provisions set forth in Articles 7 and 8 of this Agreement. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as a guarantee of employment for any specific duration, and no provision of this Agreement shall be interpreted to alter the at-will nature of Executive's employment except as expressly provided in Articles 7 and 8 with respect to severance benefits and related protections.
Section 3.2 — Effective Date
This Agreement shall become effective on the Effective Date. The Effective Date is contingent upon the satisfactory completion of: (a) a background verification and reference check process, which shall be conducted by the Company promptly following execution of this Agreement; and (b) confirmation of Executive's authorization to work in the United States, as required by applicable immigration and employment laws. The Company shall use commercially reasonable efforts to complete such verification processes expeditiously. If the background verification or reference check reveals information that, in the reasonable judgment of the Company, is materially inconsistent with Executive's representations herein or would disqualify Executive from employment, the Company may rescind this Agreement prior to the Effective Date upon written notice to Executive.
ARTICLE 4 — COMPENSATION
Section 4.1 — Base Salary
The Company shall pay Executive an annualized Base Salary of Four Hundred Forty Thousand Dollars ($440,000), payable in accordance with the Company's standard payroll practices as in effect from time to time (currently semi-monthly installments), less applicable withholdings and deductions as required by law.
Executive's Base Salary shall be reviewed on an annual basis by the Compensation Committee, typically in connection with the Company's annual compensation review cycle, and any increase shall be in the sole discretion of the Compensation Committee. Notwithstanding the foregoing, Executive's Base Salary shall not be decreased below Four Hundred Forty Thousand Dollars ($440,000) per annum without Executive's prior written consent. Any such decrease imposed without Executive's written consent shall constitute Good Reason under Section 7.3 of this Agreement.
Section 4.2 — Annual Performance Bonus
Section 4.2(a) — Target Bonus.  Executive shall be eligible to earn an annual performance bonus with a target of fifty-five percent (55%) of Base Salary (the "Bonus Target"), which, based on the initial Base Salary of $440,000, equals Two Hundred Forty-Two Thousand Dollars ($242,000) per annum. The actual bonus payable for any Fiscal Year may range from zero percent (0%) to two hundred percent (200%) of the Bonus Target (i.e., a maximum annual bonus of up to Four Hundred Eighty-Four Thousand Dollars ($484,000) based on the initial Base Salary), as determined by the Compensation Committee in its reasonable discretion based upon the achievement of individual and Company performance objectives. Such performance objectives shall be established by the CEO and the Compensation Committee within thirty (30) days following the start of each Fiscal Year, and Executive shall be consulted in the development of her individual performance objectives.
Annual bonuses shall be paid no later than March 15 of the calendar year following the close of the applicable Fiscal Year, subject to Executive's continued employment with the Company through the applicable payment date, except as otherwise provided in Section 4.2(b), Section 7.5(c), and Section 8.3(b) of this Agreement.
Section 4.2(b) — Guaranteed Bonus Floor (Years 1 and 2).  Notwithstanding anything to the contrary in Section 4.2(a) or in any Company bonus plan or policy, for each of Executive's first two (2) full Fiscal Years of employment (each, a "Guarantee Period Fiscal Year"), Executive shall receive an annual bonus of not less than seventy-five percent (75%) of the Bonus Target (the "Guaranteed Bonus Floor"), which, based on the initial Bonus Target of $242,000, equals One Hundred Eighty-One Thousand Five Hundred Dollars ($181,500) per Guarantee Period Fiscal Year. The total guaranteed bonus floor exposure across both Guarantee Period Fiscal Years shall be Three Hundred Sixty-Three Thousand Dollars ($363,000) (i.e., $181,500 multiplied by two).
For the avoidance of doubt, the Guaranteed Bonus Floor establishes a minimum, not a maximum. If Executive's actual performance-based bonus, as determined by the Compensation Committee in accordance with Section 4.2(a), exceeds the Guaranteed Bonus Floor in any Guarantee Period Fiscal Year, Executive shall receive the higher amount. In no event shall the existence of the Guaranteed Bonus Floor reduce or limit Executive's eligibility to earn a bonus up to the maximum bonus percentage set forth in Section 4.2(a).
If Executive's employment commences after the start of a Fiscal Year, the Guaranteed Bonus Floor for that partial Fiscal Year shall be prorated based on the number of calendar days of Executive's employment during such Fiscal Year divided by the total number of calendar days in such Fiscal Year, and such partial Fiscal Year shall count as the first Guarantee Period Fiscal Year for purposes of this Section 4.2(b).
The Guaranteed Bonus Floor shall be payable regardless of Company or individual performance achievement and shall supersede any contrary provision of the Company's annual bonus plan, cash incentive plan, or any other compensation program or policy as applied to Executive during the Guarantee Period Fiscal Years. The Guaranteed Bonus Floor is subject only to Executive's continued employment with the Company through the applicable payment date, except that in the event of a Qualifying Termination (as defined in Section 7.1 or Section 8.3) during a Guarantee Period Fiscal Year, the unpaid portion of the Guaranteed Bonus Floor for the then-current Guarantee Period Fiscal Year shall be prorated based on the number of calendar days of Executive's employment during such Fiscal Year divided by the total number of calendar days in such Fiscal Year and shall be paid as part of the severance benefits described in Section 7.5(c) or Section 8.3(b), as applicable.
Section 4.3 — Signing Bonus
This Section 4.3 is reserved for negotiation. Executive reserves the right to request a one-time signing bonus in an amount sufficient to offset compensation forfeited by Executive as a result of her departure from Helios Data Systems, LLC, including without limitation any unvested equity awards, unpaid bonuses, and other forfeited compensation. The final amount of any signing bonus shall be determined following Executive's completion of a final accounting of all compensation forfeited in connection with her resignation from Helios, which accounting Executive shall provide to the Company in reasonable detail within ten (10) business days following execution of this Agreement. Any signing bonus agreed upon by the parties shall be payable in a lump sum within thirty (30) days following the Effective Date, less applicable withholdings, and shall be subject to repayment by Executive if Executive voluntarily resigns without Good Reason or is terminated for Cause within twelve (12) months following the Effective Date.
ARTICLE 5 — EQUITY COMPENSATION
Section 5.1 — Initial RSU Grant
Effective as of the first regularly scheduled equity grant date following the Effective Date (the "Grant Date"), the Company shall grant Executive restricted stock units with an aggregate grant-date value of Two Million Two Hundred Thousand Dollars ($2,200,000) (the "Initial RSU Grant"). The number of RSUs comprising the Initial RSU Grant shall be determined by dividing Two Million Two Hundred Thousand Dollars ($2,200,000) by the fair market value per share of the Company's common stock on the Grant Date, as determined in accordance with the Company's equity incentive plan.
The Initial RSU Grant shall vest over a four (4)-year period commencing on a vesting commencement date to be specified in the RSU Award Agreement (which shall be no later than the Effective Date), as follows:
(a) Cliff Vesting: Twenty-five percent (25%) of the total RSUs subject to the Initial RSU Grant shall vest on the first anniversary of the vesting commencement date (the "Cliff Date").
(b) Quarterly Vesting: The remaining seventy-five percent (75%) of the total RSUs shall vest in twelve (12) equal quarterly installments over the thirty-six (36)-month period following the Cliff Date, with each installment vesting on the last day of each successive three (3)-month period following the Cliff Date.
Vesting of each installment shall be subject to Executive's continued service with the Company (or an Affiliate) through the applicable vesting date, except as otherwise provided in Section 8.4 of this Agreement with respect to CIC Qualifying Terminations. The Initial RSU Grant shall be subject to the terms and conditions of the Company's equity incentive plan and a separate RSU Award Agreement to be entered into between the Company and Executive, substantially in the form attached hereto as Exhibit B, which shall incorporate the acceleration provisions of Section 8.4 and shall be consistent in all material respects with the terms of this Agreement.
Section 5.2 — Annual Equity Refresh Grants
Executive shall be eligible to receive annual equity refresh grants, the amount, type, and terms of which shall be determined by the Compensation Committee in its sole discretion, consistent with annual equity refresh grants made to other similarly situated senior vice presidents and comparable senior executives of the Company. The Company acknowledges that it intends to provide annual equity refresh grants as a regular component of executive compensation and that such grants are an important element of Executive's total compensation package.
Section 5.3 — Equity Plan Participation
Executive shall be eligible to participate in all equity incentive plan(s) of the Company as in effect from time to time, including any stock option plan, restricted stock plan, stock appreciation rights plan, employee stock purchase plan, or similar equity compensation program, subject to the terms, conditions, and eligibility requirements of such plans and the approval of the Compensation Committee or the Board, as applicable.
