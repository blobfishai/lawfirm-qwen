# deep-drafting pack — Northgale commercial contracting playbook, 2026 edition

NORTHGALE SYSTEMS, INC. — COMMERCIAL CONTRACTING PLAYBOOK, 2026 EDITION (INTERNAL — LEGAL DEPARTMENT)

Scope. Applies to vendor-side services agreements where Northgale is the customer. For each term the playbook states: the Standard position; the Approved fallback (which the deal team may concede without escalation); and the Escalation trigger (which requires the stated approver before any concession).

PB-1 Renewal periods. Standard: automatic renewal in one (1) year periods. Approved fallback: NONE — multi-year renewal periods of any length are out of policy and require Chief Legal Officer (CLO) approval.

PB-2 Termination for convenience. Standard: thirty (30) days' notice. Approved fallback: up to forty-five (45) days. Escalation: any notice period above 45 days requires CLO approval.

PB-3 Wind-down. A post-termination wind-down is acceptable only if (i) capped at thirty (30) days and (ii) compensation is limited to documented out-of-pocket costs and non-cancelable third-party commitments. Full-fee wind-down compensation is out of policy.

PB-4 Payment terms. Standard: net 30. Late interest: never above 1.0% per month. Suspension-of-services rights: out of policy unless preceded by at least thirty (30) days' written cure notice, and any suspension right of any kind requires CFO sign-off.

PB-5 Limitation of liability. Standard: cap measured at fees paid or payable in the twenty-four (24) months preceding the claim. Approved fallback floor: eighteen (18) months. A twelve (12) month measurement period is below the fallback floor and requires CLO approval. The carve-outs for confidentiality, IP indemnification, and gross negligence/willful misconduct are non-negotiable; deletion of any carve-out requires joint CLO and CFO approval.

PB-6 Service credits. Standard: aggregate monthly credit cap of 20% of the monthly fee. Approved fallback floor: fifteen percent (15%). A 10% aggregate credit cap is out of policy.

Drafting note. Escalation memoranda must quantify exposure at the current monthly run-rate stated in the agreement under negotiation, showing the cap value at the standard measurement period, at the counterparty's proposed period, and at the approved fallback floor.
