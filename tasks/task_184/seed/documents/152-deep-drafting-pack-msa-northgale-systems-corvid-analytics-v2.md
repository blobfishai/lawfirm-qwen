MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT — NORTHGALE SYSTEMS CLIENT FORM v2 (EXECUTION-READY BASE)

Parties. This Master Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is made between Northgale Systems, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Customer"), and Corvid Analytics LLC, a Colorado limited liability company ("Provider"), effective as of the Effective Date stated on the signature page.

Section 1. Definitions. "Services" means the managed data-analytics services described in each Statement of Work. "Monthly Uptime" has the meaning given in Section 11. "Fees" means the amounts payable under Section 4. "Confidential Information" has the meaning given in Section 6.

Section 2. Term and Renewal.
2.1 Initial Term. The initial term runs for two (2) years from the Effective Date.
2.2 Renewal. This Agreement automatically renews for successive one (1) year renewal periods unless either party delivers written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current term.

Section 4. Fees and Payment.
4.1 Base Fee. Customer shall pay Provider a base service fee of $85,000 per month, invoiced monthly in arrears.
4.2 Payment Terms. Undisputed invoices are payable net thirty (30) days from Customer's receipt of a conforming invoice.
4.3 Late Amounts. Late undisputed amounts accrue interest at 1.0% per month or the maximum lawful rate, whichever is lower. This client form grants Provider no right to suspend Services for non-payment.

Section 6. Confidentiality. Each party shall protect the other party's Confidential Information with at least reasonable care and shall use it solely to perform this Agreement.

Section 7. Termination.
7.1 Cause. Either party may terminate for material breach not cured within thirty (30) days after written notice.
7.2 Convenience. Customer may terminate this Agreement or any Statement of Work for convenience upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice. This client form provides no wind-down period and no wind-down compensation.

Section 8. IP Indemnification. Provider shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Customer against third-party claims alleging that the Services or any deliverable infringes any patent, copyright, trademark, or trade secret.

Section 9. Limitation of Liability.
9.1 Cap. Except as stated in Section 9.2, each party's aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement is capped at the Fees paid or payable by Customer in the twenty-four (24) months preceding the first event giving rise to liability.
9.2 Carve-Outs. The Section 9.1 cap does not apply to: (a) breach of Section 6 (Confidentiality); (b) Provider's obligations under Section 8 (IP Indemnification); or (c) a party's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

Section 11. Service Levels.
11.1 Uptime. Provider shall maintain Monthly Uptime of at least 99.5%.
11.2 Credits. For each 0.1% by which Monthly Uptime falls below 99.5%, Customer receives a service credit equal to 5% of the monthly base service fee, subject to an aggregate cap of 20% of the monthly base service fee in any calendar month. Credits are Customer's sole remedy for uptime shortfalls except where the shortfall also constitutes material breach under Section 7.1.
