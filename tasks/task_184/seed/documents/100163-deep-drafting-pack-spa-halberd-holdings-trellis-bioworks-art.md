# deep-drafting pack — SPA (Halberd Holdings / Trellis BioWorks) Article VIII indemnification, execution draft v4

SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT — HALBERD HOLDINGS LP (BUYER) / TRELLIS BIOWORKS, INC. (COMPANY) AND THE SELLERS NAMED HEREIN
ARTICLE VIII (INDEMNIFICATION) — EXECUTION DRAFT v4 (SELL-SIDE CURRENT DRAFT)

Selected definitions (Article I). "Purchase Price" means $120,000,000, subject to the adjustments in Section 2.4. "Fundamental Representations" means the representations in Sections 3.1 (Organization), 3.2 (Authority), 3.3 (Capitalization) and 3.20 (Brokers). "Losses" means all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, but excluding punitive damages except to the extent payable to a third party.

8.1 Survival. The representations and warranties of the Sellers other than the Fundamental Representations survive the Closing for eighteen (18) months. The Fundamental Representations survive for six (6) years. The representations in Section 3.16 (Taxes) survive until sixty (60) days after expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. Covenants survive in accordance with their terms.

8.2 Indemnification by the Sellers. Subject to this Article VIII, the Sellers shall indemnify Buyer against Losses arising out of: (a) any breach of a representation or warranty of the Sellers; (b) any breach of a covenant of the Sellers; and (c) any Pre-Closing Taxes. Execution draft v4 contains no special indemnities.

8.3 Limitations.
(a) Deductible. The Sellers have no liability under Section 8.2(a) unless and until aggregate Losses thereunder exceed $400,000 (the "Deductible"), and then only for Losses in excess of the Deductible.
(b) Cap. The aggregate liability of the Sellers under Section 8.2(a) shall not exceed ten percent (10%) of the Purchase Price ($12,000,000); provided that Losses for breaches of Fundamental Representations are instead capped at the Purchase Price.
(c) Materiality Scrape. Materiality qualifiers are disregarded for purposes of calculating Losses (but not for determining whether a breach occurred).

8.4 Exclusive Remedy. Following the Closing, this Article VIII is the sole and exclusive remedy of the parties for the matters covered by Section 8.2, other than claims of fraud and claims for equitable relief.

8.5 Order of Recovery. Buyer shall recover first from the Escrow Amount and thereafter severally from the Sellers in proportion to their Pro Rata Shares.
