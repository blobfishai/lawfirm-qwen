# damages-computation pack — Correspondence re Mediation Fee Split and Session Logistics (Meridian v. Corvess)

CORRESPONDENCE — MEDIATION FEE SPLIT AND SESSION LOGISTICS

From: Daniel Okafor, Harlow & Trent LLP
To: Counsel of record for Corvess Industrial Group, LLC
Re: Meridian v. Corvess — mediation before Hon. Patrice W. Camden (ret.)

Counsel:

Writing to confirm logistics and the fee split for the mediation session scheduled before Judge Camden (ret.) at the Camden ADR Center.

1. Mediator's fee. The mediator's engagement letter quotes a flat session fee of $14,000 for a full-day session, inclusive of preparation time up to eight hours. Consistent with the ADR Center's standard terms and our earlier call, the parties will split the mediator's fee evenly, 50/50, with each side remitting its half directly to the ADR Center within ten days of the session.

2. Room and administrative fee. The ADR Center charges a $600 facility and administration fee for the day. We propose the same even split. If your client prefers, we are equally content for each side to pay its own breakout-room incidentals and split only the main-room charge.

3. Position statements. Judge Camden requests confidential position statements not exceeding fifteen pages, due five business days before the session. Exhibits do not count against the page limit but should be tabbed.

4. Attendance. We will have our client's chief financial officer present with full settlement authority, along with lead trial counsel. Please confirm equivalent authority on your side, as the mediator's standing order requires principals with authority in the room.

5. No effect on ledgers. For clarity, mediation fees are handled by the clients directly under our respective engagement terms; nothing in this letter is to be recorded on the litigation cost ledgers, the filings ledger, or any damages computation in the case. This letter addresses logistics only and states no position on the merits or on any stipulated figure.

Please confirm by reply that the even split and the logistics above work for your side, and we will notify the ADR Center jointly.

Regards,
Daniel Okafor
Harlow & Trent LLP, counsel for Meridian Fabrication Corp.
