# Discovery-retrieval pack — Cover purchase summary: replacement grain procurement premium (2025-05-27)

MERIDIAN GRAIN COOPERATIVE, INC. — MERCHANDISING DESK SUMMARY (PRODUCED IN DISCOVERY)
May 27, 2025
From: G. Lindqvist, Senior Grain Merchandiser
To: Oskar Brandt, CFO; Adaeze Reyes, Harwick & Sloane LLP
Re: Cover purchases to meet delivery obligations — final premium computation

1. Background. At the time the Complex 4 spoilage was discovered, the cooperative carried forward-delivery obligations to two flour millers — Askel Milling Company (Duluth) and Prairie Hearth Mills (Fargo) — deliverable May through August 2025, which had been allocated against the Complex 4 stocks. To avoid default on those contracts the desk executed cover purchases of equivalent No. 1 hard red spring wheat, 13.5 percent protein basis, in the cash market between May 9 and May 23, 2025.

2. What this summary measures. This summary states the incremental cost of cover only: the premium of the cover purchase prices over the contract benchmark prices embedded in the miller contracts, plus the freight differential arising from sourcing out-of-position bushels (primarily ex-elevator Minot and Aberdeen rather than Halden Junction). It deliberately excludes the value of the spoiled inventory itself, which is stated in the CFO's inventory valuation memorandum of May 20, 2025, so that the two components can be added without double counting.

3. Computation. Eleven cover trades totaling 1,010,000 bushels. Weighted average cover price premium over contract benchmark: 2.06 dollars per bushel, for 2,080,600 dollars. Freight differential on out-of-position origination, net of saved Halden Junction elevation: 329,400 dollars. Both figures are drawn from executed trade confirmations and rate confirmations at tabs A and B.

4. Result. The final cover-purchase premium is 2,410,000 dollars. The desk considers this figure final; all trades are settled and no further cover is anticipated for the affected delivery windows.

5. Notes. Both miller counterparties accepted the substituted deliveries without claim. Demurrage was avoided. The desk makes no legal characterization of recoverability; that is for counsel.

G. Lindqvist
Senior Grain Merchandiser, Meridian Grain Cooperative, Inc.
