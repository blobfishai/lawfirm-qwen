# Discovery-retrieval pack — Inventory valuation memo: spoiled grain lots, Silo Complex 4 (2025-05-20)

MERIDIAN GRAIN COOPERATIVE, INC. — INTERNAL MEMORANDUM (PRIVILEGED PORTIONS REDACTED FOR PRODUCTION)
May 20, 2025
From: Oskar Brandt, Chief Financial Officer, with G. Lindqvist, Senior Grain Merchandiser
To: Adaeze Reyes, Harwick & Sloane LLP
Re: Final valuation of spoiled inventory, Silo Complex 4, Halden Junction

1. Purpose and limitation. This memorandum states the cooperative's final valuation of the grain destroyed by the humidity excursion at Silo Complex 4. This memorandum values only the destroyed inventory. It does not include replacement or cover costs, which are addressed in the separate cover purchase summary being prepared by the merchandising desk, and it does not include incidental handling, disposal or fumigation charges.

2. Affected inventory. Storage lots 41 through 58, comprising 1,040,000 bushels of No. 1 hard red spring wheat, 13.5 percent protein basis, placed into the Complex 4 galleries between September 2024 and February 2025. State grain inspection on May 6-8, 2025 graded the affected lots as sample grade due to moisture damage, heat damage and mold, with the inspection certificates attached at tab B.

3. Methodology. Each lot is valued at the Chicago Board of Trade reference price for the applicable delivery month as of the grading date, adjusted for the cooperative's historical Halden Junction basis, protein premium, and freight to the primary delivery market, less the realized salvage value obtained at auction for the sample-grade material (feed and industrial use). Lot-by-lot detail appears at tab C. No lost-profit, interest or consequential element is included in this figure.

4. Result. The final spoiled-inventory loss, net of salvage, is 6,240,000 dollars. This figure replaces all earlier working numbers circulated internally while grading and salvage were pending, and it is the number the cooperative will stand behind in this litigation for the destroyed-inventory component.

5. Reliance. The merchandising desk's price and basis worksheets, the inspection certificates, and the salvage auction settlement sheets are maintained in the matter file and are available for expert review.

Oskar Brandt, CFO
Meridian Grain Cooperative, Inc.
