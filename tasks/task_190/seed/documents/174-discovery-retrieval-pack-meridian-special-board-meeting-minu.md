# Discovery-retrieval pack — Meridian special board meeting minutes: settlement authority vote (2025-08-01)

MERIDIAN GRAIN COOPERATIVE, INC.
MINUTES OF A SPECIAL MEETING OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
August 1, 2025 — 2:00 PM CT — cooperative offices, Halden Junction, and by telephone

Present: President and Chair A. Sundstrom; Directors M. Halvorsen, T. Ruiz, C. Bergstrom, D. Okonkwo, F. Lindahl, P. Vasquez. Also present by invitation: O. Brandt, CFO; A. Reyes of Harwick & Sloane LLP, litigation counsel.

1. Call to order; notice; quorum. The Chair called the special meeting to order at 2:03 PM. The Secretary confirmed that notice of the special meeting was duly given and that a quorum of seven directors was present.

2. Purpose. The Chair stated that the special meeting was called to consider (a) the settlement offer received from Talvern Logistics Group LLC in the pending litigation, and (b) ratification of the amended engagement terms with litigation counsel.

3. Settlement offer. Ms. Reyes reported that by letter dated July 22, 2025, counsel for Talvern conveyed an offer of 2,100,000 dollars in full settlement of all claims, conditioned on a complete release and containing no admission of liability, open for twenty-one days. Ms. Reyes reviewed the offer against the damages components previously reported to the Board and answered directors' questions concerning litigation risk, expected costs through trial, and the spoliation issues under investigation. Director Halvorsen moved that the cooperative accept the offer as presented; Director Ruiz seconded the motion for purposes of discussion. After discussion, the Chair called the question. THE MOTION FAILED, two votes in favor (Halvorsen, Ruiz) and five opposed (Sundstrom, Bergstrom, Okonkwo, Lindahl, Vasquez). No settlement authority was granted, and the Board directed counsel to communicate the rejection of the offer and to proceed with the litigation, including the contemplated spoliation motion practice.

4. Ratification of amended engagement terms. On motion duly made and seconded, the Board voted 7-0 to ratify the First Amendment to the engagement letter with Harwick & Sloane LLP dated June 2, 2025, including the revised litigation fee budget of 1,975,000 dollars through trial stated therein.

5. Adjournment. The meeting adjourned at 3:26 PM.

Respectfully submitted,
C. Bergstrom, Secretary
