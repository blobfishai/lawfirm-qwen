# MAUD deal points pack — Halcyon Therapeutics merger agreement conformed provisions — MAE definition, interim operating covenant and non-solicitation (Sections 1.1, 5.1, 5.4)

CONFORMED PROVISIONS MEMORANDUM — Halcyon Therapeutics / Meridian Compass Agreement and Plan of Merger dated June 2, 2026. Sections 1.1 (selected definition), 5.1 (interim operating covenant) and 5.4 (non-solicitation). Prepared by Ellsworth & Crane LLP knowledge management for the annual public-target deal-points survey. Quoted language is verbatim from the execution version.

1. Definition of "Company Material Adverse Effect" (Section 1.1). "Company Material Adverse Effect" means any change, event, occurrence or effect that has had, or would reasonably be expected to have, a material adverse effect on the business, financial condition or results of operations of the Company and its Subsidiaries, taken as a whole; provided, however, that no change, event, occurrence or effect arising out of or resulting from any of the following shall constitute, or be taken into account in determining whether there has been, a Company Material Adverse Effect: (i) general economic, credit, capital or financial market conditions; (ii) conditions generally affecting the industries in which the Company and its Subsidiaries operate; (iii) any change in Law, GAAP or the interpretation or enforcement thereof; (iv) any epidemic, pandemic or other public health event (including COVID-19 and any COVID-19 Measures or other governmental responses thereto); (v) any acts of war, armed hostilities, sabotage, terrorism, natural disasters or other force majeure events; (vi) the negotiation, execution, announcement or pendency of this Agreement; (vii) any failure by the Company to meet any internal or published projections, forecasts or revenue or earnings predictions (it being understood that the underlying causes of any such failure may be taken into account unless otherwise excluded by this definition); or (viii) any action taken at the written request of Parent or expressly required by this Agreement; provided, further, that any change, event, occurrence or effect referred to in clauses (i), (ii), (iv) or (v) above shall be taken into account in determining whether a Company Material Adverse Effect has occurred to the extent it has a disproportionate impact on the Company and its Subsidiaries, taken as a whole, relative to other participants in the industries in which the Company and its Subsidiaries operate. Survey-grid note: the pandemic carveout in clause (iv) IS subject to the disproportionate-impact modifier; the change-in-Law carveout in clause (iii) is not.

2. Interim Operating Covenant (Section 5.1). From the date of this Agreement until the Effective Time, except as consented to in writing by Parent, the Company shall, and shall cause each of its Subsidiaries to, conduct its business in all material respects in the ordinary course of business consistent with past practice, and shall use commercially reasonable efforts to preserve substantially intact its business organization and its material business relationships.

3. Non-Solicitation; Fiduciary Exception; Matching Rights (Section 5.4). From and after the date of this Agreement, the Company shall not, and shall cause its Subsidiaries and Representatives not to, directly or indirectly solicit, initiate or knowingly encourage or facilitate any Acquisition Proposal. Notwithstanding the foregoing, at any time prior to receipt of the Company Stockholder Approval, if the Company receives a bona fide written Acquisition Proposal that did not result from a breach of this Section 5.4, and the Company Board determines in good faith, after consultation with outside legal counsel and its financial advisor, that such Acquisition Proposal constitutes or would reasonably be expected to lead to a Superior Proposal and that the failure to take such action would be inconsistent with the directors' fiduciary duties under applicable Law, the Company may furnish information pursuant to an Acceptable Confidentiality Agreement and participate in negotiations with the Person making such proposal. Prior to effecting any Adverse Recommendation Change or terminating to accept a Superior Proposal, the Company shall give Parent four (4) Business Days' prior written notice and shall negotiate in good faith with Parent during such period (the "Match Period"); a new notice with an additional three (3) Business Days' Match Period is required for each material amendment to the financial terms of such Superior Proposal.
