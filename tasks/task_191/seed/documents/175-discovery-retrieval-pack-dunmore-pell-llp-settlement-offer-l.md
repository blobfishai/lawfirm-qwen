Re: Meridian Grain Cooperative, Inc. v. Talvern Logistics Group LLC, No. 0:25-cv-01877 — offer of compromise

Dear Ms. Reyes:

This letter is a confidential offer of compromise made solely to avoid the burden and expense of continued litigation. Nothing in it is or may be construed as an admission of liability, fault, or the truth of any allegation in the complaint, all of which Talvern Logistics Group LLC continues to deny.

On behalf of Talvern, we are authorized to offer 2,100,000 dollars, payable within thirty days of execution of a settlement agreement, in full and final settlement of all claims that were or could have been asserted in the above-captioned action, conditioned on: (a) a complete mutual release of all claims arising from or relating to the Warehousing and Climate Services Agreement and the storage of the cooperative's grain at Silo Complex 4; (b) dismissal of the action with prejudice, each party to bear its own fees and costs; (c) withdrawal of the cooperative's pending preservation and spoliation correspondence and any contemplated motion practice thereon; and (d) customary confidentiality and non-disparagement terms.

In our client's view this offer should be measured against the genuine disputes in the case, including causation of the claimed grading outcomes, the cooperative's own monitoring obligations under Section 4.7 of the agreement, and the substantial cost both parties face through expert discovery and trial.

This offer remains open for twenty-one days from the date of this letter, after which it is withdrawn without further notice. It may be accepted only in a writing signed by our client.

Very truly yours,
Jonas Pell
Dunmore Pell LLP
Counsel for Talvern Logistics Group LLC
